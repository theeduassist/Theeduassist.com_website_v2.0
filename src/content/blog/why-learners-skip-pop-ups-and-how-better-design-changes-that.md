## **What Is a Pop Up?**

A pop up is a small overlay element that appears on top of existing content to deliver a message, instruction, or interaction, without pulling the user away from their current task.

Pop ups are designed to:

- Deliver real time information
- Guide users step by step
- Reinforce learning
- Prompt quick actions or responses

Unlike full pages, pop ups are contextual, focused, and brief. They appear when needed, and disappear once the task is complete.

## **What Is Pop Up Editing?**

Pop up editing is the process of refining pop up content and design to make sure it is:

- Clear and easy to understand
- Visually clean and on brand
- Aligned with learning or business goals
- Convenient and easy to navigate

Editing goes far beyond grammar. It includes:

- Breaking down complex information
- Improving formatting and clarity
- Adjusting timing and placement
- Maintaining consistency across platforms
- Removing clutter to sharpen the message

## **Types of Pop Ups**

Understanding the different types of pop ups helps ensure they are designed correctly.

**Informational Pop Ups** Quick explanations, definitions, or clarifications.

**Instructional Pop Ups** Step by step guidance during tasks or learning activities.

**Interactive Pop Ups** Decision points, branching scenarios, or quizzes that engage the learner directly.

**Feedback Pop Ups** Immediate responses to user actions, whether correct, incorrect, or improvement focused.

**Notification and Alert Pop Ups** System messages, alerts, confirmations, or reminders.

Each type serves a distinct purpose and needs careful design and editing.
