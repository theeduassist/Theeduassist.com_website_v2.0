Setting up Kajabi for the first time can feel like a lot. You may see options for websites, courses, offers, payments, email marketing, landing pages, automations, and analytics before you even create your first product.

The good news is that you do not need to configure everything on day one.

A successful **Kajabi account setup** starts with the essential business settings and then moves toward your website, payment system, digital product, offer, email marketing, and customer journey. Taking this approach can help you avoid common problems before launching your first course or digital product.

Kajabi's current setup guidance recommends completing foundational settings such as business information, branding, domain, payments, email, website details, and testing before moving fully into your launch.

This guide explains how beginners can set up Kajabi step by step and prepare a professional foundation for an online course or knowledge-based business.

## What Is Kajabi?

Kajabi is an all-in-one platform designed for people who want to create, market, and sell digital products and services.

Its features can support online courses, memberships, coaching programs, websites, landing pages, email marketing, offers, payments, sales funnels, and customer management.

For educators, coaches, consultants, course creators, and training businesses, this means several parts of the customer journey can be managed from one platform.

Kajabi's beginner tutorial recommends starting with the core account settings before building products, including payments, domains, marketing settings, and website details.

For readers who are still deciding whether the platform is suitable for their needs, TheEduAssist also covers [Is Kajabi Easy to Use?](https://www.theeduassist.com/blog/is-kajabi-easy-to-use/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

## What Do You Need Before Setting Up Kajabi?

Before you start configuring your account, prepare the basic information and assets you are likely to need.

These can include:

- Business or brand name
- Professional email address
- Logo
- Favicon
- Brand colors
- Website domain
- Business contact information
- Course or product idea
- Payment account
- Basic product description
- Customer or audience information

You do not need a completely finished business before starting. However, having these essentials ready can make the setup process smoother.

If you already own a domain, keep access to your domain registrar available because you may need to modify DNS settings when connecting it to Kajabi.

## Step 1: Create Your Kajabi Account

The first step is creating your Kajabi account and entering the information requested during registration.

After signing in, you will arrive at your dashboard. This becomes the central workspace for managing your website, products, customers, marketing activities, and other parts of your business.

At this stage, avoid immediately uploading dozens of course lessons.

Instead, start with your account settings.

Kajabi's current setup guide explains that completing foundational settings early can prevent problems involving branding, email deliverability, payments, and other launch requirements.

## Step 2: Configure Your Business Information

Start by completing your business information.

Depending on your business, this can include:

- Business name
- Logo
- Favicon
- Contact information
- Business details

Your branding can appear across different customer-facing areas of your Kajabi account.

This is important because consistency helps visitors recognize your business across your website, emails, checkout pages, and learning environment.

For example, if your website uses one business name but your emails display a different sender name, customers may become confused.

Set your core branding once and then use it consistently.

## Step 3: Connect Your Custom Domain

A custom domain gives your online learning business a branded web address.

For example:

**[yourbusiness.com](http://yourbusiness.com)**

Instead of relying entirely on a platform-generated address, a custom domain gives you a web identity that can remain connected to your business as your audience grows.

Kajabi's current setup checklist recommends configuring a custom domain during the early setup process. DNS changes can require some time before they become fully active.

Before connecting the domain:

1. Confirm that you own the domain.
2. Make sure you can access your domain registrar.
3. Identify where DNS records can be managed.
4. Follow Kajabi's current domain instructions.
5. Test the domain after configuration.

Do not wait until launch day to complete this step.

## Step 4: Set Up Your Payment System

If you intend to sell an online course, membership, coaching program, or digital product, payment processing is essential.

Kajabi's beginner documentation recommends setting up payment integrations early. Depending on your account and location, payment options and available integrations can vary. Kajabi's documentation discusses payment connections such as Stripe and PayPal.

Your payment system should be tested before you start promoting a product.

Check the complete purchasing journey:

**Product → Offer → Checkout → Payment → Confirmation → Product Access**

TheEduAssist also has a dedicated guide covering [What Payment Methods Does Kajabi Accept?](https://www.theeduassist.com/blog/what-payment-methods-does-kajabi-accept/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

This can be useful if you need more information about payment options before configuring your checkout.

## Step 5: Configure Your Email Settings

Email is another important part of your setup.

Your email settings affect how customers receive communications from your business.

Configure the relevant information, including:

- Sender name
- Sender email
- Business information
- Email branding
- Required business address information
- Email domain settings where applicable

Kajabi's beginner guide recommends configuring email settings after handling the basic payment and domain setup.

You should also send test emails to yourself.

Check:

- Sender name
- Subject line
- Formatting
- Links
- Mobile display
- Footer information
- Call-to-action buttons

A customer should immediately understand who sent the message and why they received it.

## Step 6: Build Your Basic Website

Once your foundational settings are ready, you can begin building your website.

You do not need twenty pages to launch.

A simple starting structure could include:

- Home
- About
- Products or Courses
- Contact
- Blog
- Privacy Policy
- Terms and Conditions

Your homepage should quickly communicate:

**Who are you?**

**Who do you help?**

**What do you offer?**

**What should the visitor do next?**

Kajabi's beginner resources explain that its website builder provides customizable themes and allows creators to build websites without requiring traditional coding skills.

For TheEduAssist's audience, the website should also support the educational journey rather than simply act as an online brochure.

## Step 8: Create an Offer

One important concept for beginners is understanding the difference between a product and an offer.

The **product** contains the content or experience.

The **offer** connects that product with pricing and checkout.

For example:

**Product:** SEO Fundamentals Course

**Offer:** $99 one-time payment

You could potentially create another offer for the same product using a different pricing structure, depending on your business model.

Kajabi's current product-launch guide describes an Offer as the pricing layer connecting the product with the checkout experience.

Before publishing an offer, make sure your pricing, payment method, product access, and customer messaging are clear.

## Step 9: Create a Sales or Landing Page

Your sales page explains the product to potential customers.

A useful sales page should answer questions such as:

- What problem does the product solve?
- Who is it for?
- What will the learner receive?
- What topics are included?
- How does access work?
- What does it cost?
- What happens after purchase?

Keep the structure easy to scan.

Use clear headings, short paragraphs, relevant visuals, and strong calls to action.

Avoid making the page complicated simply because Kajabi gives you many customization options.

## Step 10: Set Up Email Marketing and Automation

Once your product and offer are ready, connect your email marketing system.

A simple customer journey might look like:

**Visitor**

→ Landing Page

→ Email Signup

→ Welcome Email

→ Educational Content

→ Product Offer

→ Checkout

→ Purchase

→ Customer Onboarding

This is where automation can save time.

For example, someone who downloads a free resource could receive a welcome email followed by educational messages and an invitation to explore a paid course.

A lead form allows visitors to join your email list.

The purpose is not simply to collect as many email addresses as possible.

The goal is to attract people who are genuinely interested in the problem your product solves.

Kajabi's setup guidance includes forms and pipelines among the foundational tools creators can configure before launching.

## Step 13: Optimize Basic SEO Settings

SEO should be considered during your initial website setup.

Start with the basics:

- Descriptive page titles
- Relevant meta descriptions
- Clear URLs
- Logical heading structure
- Helpful content
- Internal links
- Descriptive image alt text
- Mobile-friendly pages

Do not repeat the same keyword unnaturally throughout your content.

Instead, answer the searcher's question clearly and organize the information so both readers and search engines can understand the page.

Your blog should also have a clear relationship with your products.

For example, an article about creating an online course can naturally link to your course-development resources or relevant product page.

Once everything works, you can prepare your launch.

- Who is the product for?

## Common Kajabi Setup Mistakes

### 1. Building Everything at Once

Beginners sometimes try to configure every feature immediately.

Start with your essential customer journey and expand later.

### 2. Forgetting Mobile Users

Always check your website and important pages on mobile devices.

### 3. Launching Without Testing

A small checkout or email problem can create unnecessary friction for customers.

Test the complete journey before launch.

### 4. Ignoring Branding

Use consistent logos, colors, fonts, and messaging across your website and customer communications.

### 5. Creating Too Many Automations

Automation is useful when it solves a real workflow problem. Too many complicated sequences can make your system difficult to understand and maintain.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Kajabi suitable for beginners?

Kajabi can be used by beginners, although its range of features means there is a learning curve. Starting with the basic settings, website, product, offer, and customer journey makes the platform easier to approach.

### What should I set up first in Kajabi?

Start with business settings, branding, payments, domain, email settings, and site details. Kajabi's own beginner guidance recommends handling these foundational settings before moving further into product creation.

### Do I need a custom domain for Kajabi?

A custom domain is not necessarily required to begin, but it can provide a more professional branded website address. If you already own a domain, configuring it early can help avoid delays later.

### Can I create an online course with Kajabi?

Yes. Kajabi supports digital products including online courses, memberships, coaching, and other knowledge-based products.

### Should I test Kajabi before launching?

Yes. Test your website, forms, emails, checkout, payment process, product access, and customer journey before sending real visitors to your offer.

### Can Kajabi support blogging and SEO?

Yes. Kajabi provides blogging functionality with SEO and sharing settings for blog pages and individual posts.

## Final Thoughts

A successful Kajabi setup is less about activating every available feature and more about creating a reliable foundation.

Start with your business information, branding, domain, payment system, email settings, and website. Then create your first product and offer, build a simple customer journey, and test everything before launch.

Once that foundation works, you can expand into blogging, advanced automation, additional products, email campaigns, and other growth activities.

For educators, coaches, consultants, creators, and training businesses, this structured approach can make Kajabi easier to manage while keeping the focus on the learner and customer experience.
