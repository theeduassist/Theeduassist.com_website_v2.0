## Meeting Enterprise Procurement Standards

Enterprise accounts payable departments follow strict disbursement guidelines. Timberline invoicing incorporates essential B2B features:

### 1. Itemized Purchase Orders
Invoices include corporate billing fields, custom PO numbers, corporate legal entity designations, and employer identification numbers (EIN) required for corporate tax deductions.

### 2. Instant Bank Verification via Plaid
When a corporate manager clicks the payment link on an invoice, they can authenticate their corporate checking account securely using Plaid, eliminating manual routing and account number typos.

### 3. Configurable Settlement and Access Provisioning
Creators can calibrate how course access is granted:
* **Immediate Trust Provisioning:** For established enterprise partners, access tokens and student seats unlock the moment the bank transfer is initiated.
* **Settlement-Gated Provisioning:** For new clients, student account invitations remain queued until NACHA network settlement completes (typically 3 to 4 business days).

## Practical Deployment: Issuing Corporate Invoices in Kajabi

To issue a professional corporate invoice with ACH payment support:

### Step 1: Open Kajabi Invoicing
Navigate to **Sales > Invoices** in your admin navigation. Click **Create New Invoice**.

### Step 2: Input Corporate Buyer Details
Enter the corporate client business name, accounts payable email, billing address, and required purchase order number.

### Step 3: Add Line Items and Seat Licenses
Select your digital course or coaching package:
* `Enterprise Leadership Masterclass - 25 Seat License` (\$7,500)
* `Executive Coaching Add-On - 4 Private Debriefs` (\$3,000)

### Step 4: Enable ACH Bank Transfer as Preferred Payment Rail
In the accepted payment methods settings, check **ACH Direct Debit**. Set payment terms (such as `Due on Receipt` or `Net 30`).

### Step 5: Deliver the Digital Invoice
Kajabi delivers a branded PDF invoice directly to the corporate accounting desk with an interactive link where they can authenticate their bank account and authorize payment in seconds.

## Best Practices for B2B Educational Invoicing

To ensure your corporate sales operations run smoothly:

* **Specify Payment Terms Explicitly:** Clearly state whether invoices are due upon receipt or on Net 15/Net 30 terms to avoid accounts payable delays.
* **Account for Banking Settlement Latency:** Communicate clearly that ACH transfers take three to five business days to clear, avoiding misunderstandings about access timing.
* **Automate Invoice Reminders:** Set up automated courteous reminder notices at 7 days and 2 days before invoice due dates to keep invoices at the top of the payment queue.
