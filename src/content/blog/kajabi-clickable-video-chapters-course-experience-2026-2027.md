## The Instructional Science: Combating Cognitive Overload

In modern instructional design, cognitive load theory emphasizes that segmenting instructional media into distinct thematic units improves information retention by up to 60%. When learners can see the structural roadmap of a lesson before it begins, their mental model for absorbing the content improves substantially.

## Technical Configuration: Adding Chapter Markers in Kajabi

Implementing chapters inside the modernized course builder is fast and straightforward:

### Method 1: In-Editor Timestamp Markers
1. Navigate to **Products > Courses** and select your curriculum outline.
2. Open the specific lesson containing your uploaded video.
3. Under the video preview player, click **Manage Chapters**.
4. Scrub to each transition point, input a concise title (such as `04:15 - Setting Up DNS Records`), and click **Add Marker**.

### Method 2: WebVTT File Upload
For enterprise academies producing hundreds of video assets using external editing suites (like Adobe Premiere, DaVinci Resolve, or Final Cut):
1. Export a standardized `.vtt` chapter track file.
2. Upload the VTT file directly into the lesson video settings.
3. Kajabi automatically maps the timing cues onto the player scrubber bar across web and mobile viewports.

## 4 Instructional Guidelines for High-Utility Chapters

To ensure your chapters enhance learning without cluttering the player interface, apply these core guidelines:

1. **Keep Chapter Titles Action-Oriented:** Write `Configuring Stripe Webhooks` rather than `Section 2`. Clear descriptions help students locate reference steps quickly.
2. **Space Chapters Every 4 to 8 Minutes:** Chapters spaced 30 seconds apart overwhelm the scrubber bar, while chapters spaced 30 minutes apart provide minimal navigational value.
3. **Use Accurate Capitalization and Formatting:** Format your timestamps consistently (`MM:SS` or `HH:MM:SS`) to prevent formatting glitches on mobile screens.
4. **Coordinate Chapters with Lesson Downloads:** Reference chapter titles directly in your worksheets, checklists, and slide handouts to create a cohesive learning journey.
