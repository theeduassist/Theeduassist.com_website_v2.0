## What is **Airbnb**?

**Airbnb** is an online marketplace connecting property owners (hosts) with travelers seeking short-term rentals spare rooms, entire homes, apartments, treehouses, or unique stays worldwide.

## **How Airbnb Works?**

**For Hosts:**

- List properties with photos, descriptions, pricing, house rules
- Guests book instantly; Airbnb handles payments (3-5% host fee)
- Superhost badges for 4.8+ ratings, fast responses

**For Guests:**

- Search by location/dates/price; read reviews
- 14% guest service fee
- 24/7 support, verified IDs, flexible cancellation

## **Airbnb Stats (2026)**

- 8+ million listings, 220+ countries
- Hosts average $14K/year per listing
- 44% global short-term rental market share
