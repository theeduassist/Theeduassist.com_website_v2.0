Teachable used to be easy. In fact, it was the top pick for new course creators. However, prices went up. Also, limits got tighter. So, many people now search for Teachable alternatives. Indeed, this search has grown fast as Teachable’s costs climb each year.

To help you navigate easily, this guide is simple: first, it shows why people leave, and next, it highlights exactly what to check in a new tool. Finally, it lists the best Teachable alternatives for 2026, so you can pick one with confidence. In short, finding the right Teachable alternative does not have to feel hard.


## **What Is the Best Teachable Alternative?**

Overall, there is no single winner. Instead, the best Teachable alternative depends on your specific needs. * **Full ownership:** LearnDash
* **All-in-one marketing:** Kajabi
* **Budget growth:** Thinkific
* **Interactive learning:** LearnWorlds
* **Corporate training:** TalentLMS
For example, solo creators often pick Thinkific or Kajabi. Meanwhile, WordPress fans like LearnDash. On the other hand, big teams pick TalentLMS or Absorb LMS. In short, the right Teachable alternative fits your own business.


## **Why Users Are Moving Away from Teachable**


### **Pricing and Revenue Considerations**

First, Teachable cut its free plan. Also, the Starter tier still takes a 7.5% platform fee on top of standard payment processing cuts. As a result, a $100 sale loses about $10.70 to combined fees. Meanwhile, higher plans skip that transaction fee, but those subscriptions cost more. For instance, on annual billing, they run $29 for Starter, $69 for Builder, and $139 for Growth.


### **Limited Customization and Ownership**

To begin with, Teachable hosts your school on its own servers. So, you cannot touch the code. In contrast, LearnDash gives full control. Thus, many creators switch to this Teachable alternative for true ownership.


### **Learning Experience Limitations**

Indeed, Teachable works well for video courses. However, it still lacks SCORM support. Notably, this matters for training teams. Likewise, it matters for schools too.


### **Corporate and Enterprise Training Needs**

Notably, Teachable has no per-user pricing. Likewise, it lacks compliance tracking. Plus, it skips single sign-on on lower plans. Consequently, most firms skip it from day one. Instead, they look at tools built just for staff training.


### **Scalability Challenges**

Even so, Teachable imposes strict product limits across its tiers. The Starter plan limits you to just 1 product, the Builder plan caps you at 5, and even the high-tier Advanced plan caps you at 100 products. Once you hit those walls, you are forced to upgrade.


## **Key Criteria for Evaluating a Teachable Alternative**

Before you switch, check each tool against these points.

* **Course tools**: builders, quizzes, drip content
* **Engagement**: live sessions, gamification, groups
* **Reporting**: data on sales and course completion
* **Integrations**: email tools, CRM, Zapier
* **Branding**: your own domain and look
* **Long-term cost**: the renewal price, not just day one
Overall, a good Teachable alternative should score well on all six points. After all, a strong Teachable alternative saves you time and money down the road.

## **The Best LMS Alternatives to Teachable**


### **LearnDash: Best for Full Ownership and WordPress Flexibility**

To start, LearnDash runs on your own site. Therefore, your data stays yours. Also, there is no fee on sales. For this reason, many call it the top Teachable alternative for full control. Furthermore, it scales well as your course list grows.

**Key Features:** course builder, gradebooks, gamification, store checkout

**Pros:** zero fees, full design freedom, strong support

**Cons:** needs some tech skill; extras cost more

**Best For:** creators who already use WordPress

**Pricing:** from $199/year


### **Kajabi: Best All-in-One Creator Platform**

In contrast, Kajabi bundles courses with email and funnels. So, you get one login for it all. As a result, this Teachable alternative cuts the need for extra tools. Plus, it saves you the hassle of stitching apps together.

**Key Features:** email automation, landing pages, funnels, and podcast tools.

**Pros:** fewer outside tools; clean templates.

**Cons:** higher cost; caps on top plans, too.

**Best For:** owners who want marketing built in.

**Pricing:** From $149/month ($119/month when billed annually); limited Kickstarter plan sometimes available for $89/month.


### **Thinkific: Best for Growing Course Businesses**

Similarly, Thinkific feels simple, much like Teachable. However, it adds groups. It also skips fees on paid plans. For this reason, it tops many lists of Teachable alternatives. Plus, it grows with you as your school expands.

**Key Features:** unlimited courses, built-in groups, and auto emails.

**Pros:** no fee cut; easy to learn.

**Cons:** email tools still feel basic.

**Best For:** creators past their first course.

**Pricing:** From $49/month ($36/mo when billed annually) up to $199/month.


### **LearnWorlds: Best for Interactive Learning Experiences**

By contrast, LearnWorlds focuses on engagement. For instance, it has interactive video and SCORM files. Among Teachable alternatives, it leads in teaching depth. Additionally, it suits anyone who values a rich learner experience.

**Key Features:** video editor, SCORM support, certificates, affiliate tools

**Pros:** strong tools; fair price

**Cons:** entry plan adds a per-sale fee

**Best For:** teachers who want deep engagement

**Pricing:** around $24–$79/month


### **TalentLMS: Best for Corporate Training**

Meanwhile, TalentLMS suits HR teams. Unlike Teachable, it handles onboarding well. So, it is a smart Teachable alternative for staff training. Additionally, it scales smoothly as a company grows.

**Key Features:** auto-setup, gamification, certificate tracking

**Pros:** clear per-user price; fast setup

**Cons:** top reports need higher tiers

**Best For:** firms training staff or partners

**Pricing:** around $69–$109/month


### **Moodle: Best Open-Source LMS**

Likewise, Moodle runs many schools worldwide. Since it is free and open, you control your data. As a free Teachable alternative, it suits schools with tech staff. Furthermore, it scales to fit any size of institution.

**Key Features:** plugin library, SCORM and xAPI, gradebook

**Pros:** free core tool; deep control

**Cons:** needs setup skill; plain look

**Best For:** schools with IT help

**Pricing:** free self-hosted; cloud from $130/year


### **Absorb LMS: Best for Enterprise Learning**

Finally, Absorb serves large firms. Specifically, it offers strict compliance tools. For big firms, it is the strongest pick on this list. Overall, it fits any firm that needs deep reporting.

**Key Features:** AI tips, multi-language support, deep reports

**Pros:** scales well; strong support

**Cons:** custom price only; too big for solo use

**Best For:** large, regulated firms

**Pricing:** custom quote, often high monthly

## **How to Successfully Migrate from Teachable**

To begin, a switch is not a full restart. Instead, follow these five steps below to move your school the smart way.

* **Save your assets.** Pull videos, files, and quizzes first.
* **Export your data.** Save student lists and progress early.
* **Rebuild your links.** Reconnect email tools and payments.
* **Test the flow.** Sign up as a fake student and check it.
* **Launch and watch.** Track results for the first month.
In the end, a clean move makes any Teachable alternative feel easy, not hard. After all, careful planning beats a rushed jump every time.


## **Frequently Asked Questions**


### **What is the best Teachable alternative for beginners?**

Overall, Thinkific suits most new users. It gives free courses with no fees, plus an easy builder. So, it is a soft first step. Ultimately, that makes it a low-risk Teachable alternative for first-timers.


### **Is LearnDash cheaper than Teachable long-term?**

Yes, in most cases. LearnDash starts at $199 a year, with no fee on sales. Teachable, in turn, often costs more once fees pile up.


### **Which platform suits corporate training best?**

Generally, TalentLMS and Absorb LMS both fit a firm’s needs. They give per-user prices and strict tools that Teachable skips. Therefore, either one beats Teachable for staff-wide rollouts.


### **Can I move my courses without losing student data?**

Yes, in most cases. Thinkific, Kajabi, and LearnWorlds all take file imports. However, quizzes and links often need a manual rebuild. Still, the process stays simple with good planning.


### **Does Teachable have a free plan in 2026?**

No, it does not. Indeed, Teachable dropped its free plan, so the cheapest path now is a paid Starter plan with a short free trial.


### **How long does a typical LMS migration take?**

Usually, a small school takes one to two weeks. However, larger schools with many courses may need a full month for a clean move. Either way, careful planning shortens the timeline.


### **Are Teachable alternatives harder to set up?**

Not really. Most tools, like Thinkific and Kajabi, use simple drag-and-drop builders. Still, LearnDash and Moodle need a bit more tech skill at first.
