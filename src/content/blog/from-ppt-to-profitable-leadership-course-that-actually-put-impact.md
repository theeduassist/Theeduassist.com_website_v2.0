## **Step-by-Step: Convert PPT to Interactive eModule Leadership Course:**

### **Step 1: Audit and Repurpose Your PPT Content (200-300 words/module)**

Start with your existing **leadership course** PPT. Use Canva or PowerPoint’s export to identify slides: e.g., “Vision Setting” (5 slides) becomes Module 1.

Research tip: Structure per Gagné’s Nine Events of Instruction (*Educational Technology & Society*, 2023)gain attention (voiceover hook), inform objectives, stimulate recall.

**Action Items:**

- Break into 5-10 min micro-modules (TalentLMS Benchmark, 2025).
- Keyword-optimize titles: “Leadership Course: Mastering Visionary Goal-Setting.”
- Export assets: Images to PNG, text to Google Docs for voiceover scripts.

### **Step 2: Choose Your Platform (Kajabi/Skoll Priority)**

For profitability, use Kajabi (your fave) or Skool. Both support interactives natively.

- **Kajabi**: Drag-drop eModules, voiceover upload, quizzes. Pricing: $149/mo, 15% revenue share optional.
- **Skool**: Community-integrated **leadership course**, $99/mo, gamification for retention.

ATD research (2025) shows platform choice drives 30% uptake pick for US/AU audiences.

### **Step 3: Build Interactivity (Core Transformation)**

Ditch slides for:

- **Quizzes/Polls**: Use Typeform embed or native tools. *Journal of Research on Technology in Education* (2024) links 70% spaced repetition to mastery.
- **Branching Scenarios**: “If your team misses targets, choose A/B/C.” ResearchGate study (2023) shows 62% decision-making improvement.
- **Drag-Drop/ Simulations**: Leadership dilemmas via H5P (free plugin).

Tools: Articulate Rise ($999/yr), Genially (free tier), or Canva Education.

### **Step 4: Add Professional Voiceover**

Voiceover humanizes your **leadership course**. Record in Audacity (free) or hire via Fiverr ($50-200/module).

Best Practices (*Distance Education*, 2023):

- Pace: 140-160 wpm.
- Tone: Conversational, pausing for emphasis.
- Script: Active voice, stories (e.g., “Recall Satya Nadella’s Microsoft turnaround…”).

AI option: ElevenLabs or [Murf.ai](http://Murf.ai) for $20/mo 90% authenticity per *EdTech Journal Directory* reviews.

Sync via Adobe Captivate or iSpring Suite.

### **Step 5: Enhance with Multimedia and AI**

- Video clips: 30-sec leadership case studies (YouTube Creative Commons).
- AI Integration: ChatGPT for scenario generation; Descript for auto-edits.
- Accessibility: Captions via [Otter.ai](http://Otter.ai) (WEF Future of Jobs emphasizes inclusive L&D).

Test for mobile: 85% learners are mobile-first (Degreed Resources, 2025).
