# Kajabi vs ClickFunnels: The Best Platform for Creators in 2026

Kajabi or ClickFunnels? It is one of the most searched platform debates in the [online course](https://www.theeduassist.com/blog/how-to-create-a-course-on-kajabi-beginners-guide/) and digital product space, and for good reason. Both are powerful; both are well-established; and both have creators who swear by them.  
But they are not built for the same person. Choosing the wrong one does not just cost money; it costs months of rebuilding. This guide gives you a straight comparison so you can make the right call the first time.  
For context on how [sales funnels](https://www.theeduassist.com/blog/how-to-sell-online-courses-that-actually-convert/) work inside Kajabi specifically, our guide on Kajabi Sales Funnel Strategy for 2026 Growth is a useful companion to this article.

## 1. What Each Platform Was Built For

### Kajabi

- Built specifically for knowledge creators: course sellers, coaches, and membership site owners
- All-in-one: courses, email marketing, website, community, and automations in one platform
- Core philosophy: you should not need five different tools stitched together
- Thinks in terms of student journeys and long-term subscriber relationships

### ClickFunnels

Built for marketers and entrepreneurs focused on conversion-first selling

- Core strength is the funnel builder: landing pages, order forms, upsells, and downsells
- Does not host courses with the same depth Kajabi offers
- Thinks in terms of moving cold prospects through a buying sequence, fast  
That distinction matters more than any individual feature. Kajabi is a course creator’s operating system. ClickFunnels is a marketer’s conversion engine.

Kajabi has a native community feature built in. ClickFunnels 2.0 does not offer this natively.

Kajabi provides clean checkout pages with one-click upsells. ClickFunnels 2.0 offers more advanced, multi-step funnel checkouts.

### Automation

Kajabi uses a visual automations builder. ClickFunnels 2.0 relies on workflow automations.

### Analytics

Kajabi focuses on product and email data. ClickFunnels 2.0 provides strong funnel conversion data.

### Affiliate Programme

Both Kajabi and ClickFunnels 2.0 include a built-in affiliate program.  
The short version: Kajabi wins on what happens after the sale. ClickFunnels wins on the mechanics of the sale itself.

## 3. Pricing in 2026

### Entry Level

Kajabi's Basic plan starts at $149/month, while ClickFunnels 2.0's Basic plan starts at $97/month.

### Mid Tier

Kajabi's Growth plan is priced at $199/month, while ClickFunnels 2.0's Pro plan is priced at $197/month.

### Top Tier

Kajabi's Pro plan costs $399/month, while ClickFunnels 2.0's Funnel Hacker plan costs $297/month.

### Free Trial

Both Kajabi and ClickFunnels 2.0 offer a 14-day free trial.

### Transaction Fees

Neither Kajabi nor ClickFunnels 2.0 charges transaction fees.  
ClickFunnels looks cheaper at entry level. But here is the real calculation:

- Kajabi Basic includes email marketing, full website, course hosting, and community tools
- ClickFunnels Basic does not include a course platform, a full website, or robust email marketing
- Most ClickFunnels users add a separate email tool ($30 to $100/month) and a course platform on top
- Once those are added, the total cost often meets or exceeds Kajabi’s pricing  
The right question is not which platform is cheaper. It is which platform replaces the most tools for your business model.

## 4. Funnel Building: Where They Differ Most

### Kajabi Funnels

- Called ‘Pipelines’; includes pre-built templates for opt-ins, launches, webinars, and coaching
- Visual builder; easy to set up for most creators without technical experience
- Connects directly to email sequences, products, and automations inside the same platform
- One-click upsells and order bumps added in recent updates
- Limitation: A/B split testing on pages is limited compared to ClickFunnels
- Limitation: Complex multi-step conditional funnel paths can feel restrictive  
For a deeper look at how Kajabi email and automation connect inside funnels, read our article on Kajabi Email Campaigns: Boosting Opens, Clicks and Course Sales.

### ClickFunnels Funnels

- Built from the ground up as a funnel tool; it shows in every part of the interface
- More page types, deeper split testing, and more conditional funnel branching than Kajabi
- Multi-step checkout flows and dynamic upsell paths handled more elegantly
- Limitation: The post-purchase experience is thinner; course delivery is not its strength
- Many users end up connecting ClickFunnels to a separate course platform, adding cost and complexity

## 5. Who Should Use Which Platform

### Choose Kajabi If:

- You sell online courses, coaching programmes, or memberships and want one tool for everything
- You want built-in email marketing without paying for a separate platform
- Student experience, course completion, and community matter to your business model
- You prefer a cleaner, more contained system over maximum funnel customisation
- You are building a long-term education or coaching brand

### Choose ClickFunnels If:

- Your primary focus is high-volume lead generation and funnel conversion optimisation
- You sell physical products, supplements, or info products with complex upsell paths
- You need advanced A/B testing on funnel and landing pages
- You already have a course platform and only need a powerful front-end funnel tool
- You are an experienced marketer who wants granular control over every conversion step

## 6. Integrations and Ecosystem

### Kajabi

- Integrates with Zapier, Stripe, PayPal, Google Analytics, and Facebook Pixel
- Designed to keep everything inside one ecosystem; most creators never need to go outside it
- Less flexible for complex third-party tech stacks; intentionally contained

### ClickFunnels

- Open integration approach; connects comfortably with a wide range of CRMs, email tools, and payment processors
- Strong choice if you have an existing tech stack you want to preserve
- More setup time and ongoing management when combining multiple tools

Final Thoughts  
Kajabi and ClickFunnels are not really competing for the same customer, even though they appear in the same searches.  
If you are building an online education or coaching business: Kajabi is the stronger fit  
If you are running high-volume funnels with complex conversion paths: ClickFunnels gives you more firepower  
If budget is tight: factor in every tool you would need alongside ClickFunnels before assuming it is cheaper  
The most common mistake is choosing the tool that sounds more sophisticated rather than the one that matches the actual shape of your business. Start with where you make money and what your customer experience needs to look like; the right platform becomes obvious from there.

## FAQs

Can I migrate from ClickFunnels to Kajabi?  
Yes. Kajabi supports migration and there are specialist agencies that handle it. The main things to move are your contact list, email sequences, course content, and funnel pages. Most migrations take one to four weeks depending on the size of the operation.  
Does Kajabi have a funnel builder like ClickFunnels?  
Kajabi also has a Pipelines feature that acts as a funnel builder with templates for opt-ins, launches, and webinars. It lacks the complex multi-step logic of ClickFunnels, but it has everything most course creators need. Plus it connects directly into Kajabi’s email and product systems with no extra setup.  
Which platform is better for beginners?  
If you’re a newbie selling your first course, Kajabi is the more practical place to start. ClickFunnels is faster to get a single funnel live but requires more tools to do it. Kajabi does more upfront, but keeps everything in one place so it’s less complex as you grow.  
Kajabi vs Click Funnels: which platform should I use?  
If you’re selling online courses, coaching or memberships, and want one platform that does it all from email marketing to course delivery, Kajabi is the better option. If you want to focus on building high-converting sales funnels and don’t mind managing separate tools for course hosting and email on the side, ClickFunnels is the stronger choice.
