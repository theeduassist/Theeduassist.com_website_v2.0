## The 3-Tier Offer Stacking Blueprint

To build a high-converting multi-bump checkout, structure your add-ons into complementary categories:

### 1. The Accessibility Add-On (\$17 to \$27)
* **Value Proposition:** Makes consuming the material easier and faster.
* **Examples:** Downloadable MP3 audio tracks, full lecture transcripts, or an interactive mobile Notion study dashboard.

### 2. The Implementation Shortcut (\$37 to \$57)
* **Value Proposition:** Saves hours of manual execution time.
* **Examples:** Fillable Canva graphics, pre-coded web templates, legal agreement contracts, or prompt engineering libraries.

### 3. The Community or Direct Access Add-On (\$67 to \$97)
* **Value Proposition:** Provides proximity and personalized guidance.
* **Examples:** A 30-day trial in your VIP community, an invitation to a live cohort kickoff call, or an asynchronous project review.

## Technical Configuration: Stacking Bumps in Kajabi

Configuring stacked order bumps inside your offer settings takes only a few minutes:

### Step 1: Open Your Primary Offer
Navigate to **Sales > Offers** and click on your main product offer. Scroll down to the **Order Bumps** section.

### Step 2: Add Secondary and Tertiary Bumps
Click **Add Order Bump**. You can now add multiple distinct offers:
* Assign each bump its own product entitlement or permission tag.
* Craft a concise, benefit-driven headline for each offer (e.g., `Save 10+ Hours: Add the Ready-to-Use Notion Template Vault for Just $37`).
* Upload an eye-catching 400x400 thumbnail image showcasing the visual mockup of the asset.

### Step 3: Organize Visual Spacing and Contrast
Ensure each order bump box features distinct border styling and a clear checkbox. The checkout summary sidebar updates its line-item breakdown in real time as buyers check or uncheck options.

## Best Practices for High-Converting Multiple Bumps

To keep your checkout converting at peak rates without overwhelming your customers:

* **Cap Stacks at Three Offers:** Presenting two or three bumps provides great variety; presenting five or six creates decision fatigue that can hurt core checkout conversions.
* **Keep Descriptions Under 35 Words:** A checkout box is not a long-form sales letter. Focus squarely on the immediate practical benefit.
* **Check Mobile Viewports Carefully:** Ensure that checkboxes remain easy to tap with one thumb on small smartphone screens without accidental misclicks.
