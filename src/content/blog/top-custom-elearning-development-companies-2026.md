## How to Select the Right eLearning Vendor for Your Project

Before issuing an RFP or signing a Statement of Work, assess your project against these 4 core questions:

1. **What is your compliance requirement?** If your project serves public sector staff, government departments, or accredited institutions, verify that the vendor provides **VPAT 2.4 documentation and WCAG 2.1 AA audits**.
2. **What LMS infrastructure are you targeting?** Ensure the vendor provides verified SCORM Cloud sandbox test reports before final milestone sign-off.
3. **What is the true cost structure?** Avoid open-ended hourly contracts. Insist on **guaranteed, fixed-milestone Statements of Work (SOW)** that clearly define revisions, deliverable formats, and post-launch warranties.
4. **Does the vendor protect your IP?** Require a bilateral NDA guaranteeing that your proprietary curriculum and internal data are never ingested by public AI engines.
