| English Term | Spanish Dashboard Equivalent | Functional Context |

| **Products** | *Productos* | Course curricula, coaching programs, community spaces, and podcasts. |

Having standardized, professionally translated terminology ensures that team training documentation and standard operating procedures (SOPs) remain consistent across your company.
