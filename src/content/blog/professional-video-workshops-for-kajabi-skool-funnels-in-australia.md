**Kajabi** is ideal if you want a complete “business‑in‑a‑box” for your video workshops. In Australia, coaches and educators use Kajabi to host evergreen courses, mini‑courses, and multi‑lesson video workshops inside a single dashboard.

With Kajabi you can:

* Upload and structure **video workshops** into modules and lessons.
* Add quizzes, certificates, and drip content to keep learners engaged.
* Build automated **sales funnels** using landing pages, checkout, upsells, and order bumps.
This makes Kajabi perfect for Australian experts who want to:

* Turn one‑off trainings into **recurring Kajabi memberships**.

## **Building Interactive Video Workshops Inside Skool**

* Define clear **learning outcomes**
* Break content into **micro-lessons (5–15 minutes)**
* Use frameworks: Problem → Solution → Action

To create professional workshops:

* High-quality audio (lapel or condenser mic)
* Clean visuals (screen + face cam)
* Scripted delivery (clear and concise)

### **Recommended Formats:**

* Talking head (authority building)
* Screen tutorials (skills training)
* Hybrid (most effective)
