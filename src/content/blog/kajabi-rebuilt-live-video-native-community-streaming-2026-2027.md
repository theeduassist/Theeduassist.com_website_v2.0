## The Technical Transition: External Plugins vs Native WebRTC

Understanding the engineering overhaul reveals why the rebuilt streaming engine delivers a vastly superior experience for both instructors and learners:

```
[Legacy Third-Party Live Integration Model]
Instructor Launches External Tool ──> Sends Zoom/YouTube URL to Community
   ├── Student Must Download Desktop Application or Log In Separately
   ├── High Latency (15 to 30 second delay on live chat interactions)
   ├── Post-Call Friction: Instructor Must Download Recording, Wait for Encoding,
   │   and Manually Re-Upload File to Course Library
   └── Result: Fragmented Experience, Lost Community Continuity

[Rebuilt Native Kajabi Live Video Stack (Dispatch Architecture)]
Instructor Clicks "Go Live" Inside Kajabi Community Studio
   ├── Broadcasts via Ultra-Low Latency In-House WebRTC (< 1 Second Lag)
   ├── Students Watch Directly Inside Community Web View or Mobile App (Zero Downloads)
   ├── Real-Time Synchronized Chat, Emoji Reactions & Screen Sharing
   └── Instant Archive: Stream Automatically Transcodes into Unified Media Library
   └── Result: Frictionless Participation, Higher Live Retention, Zero Post-Call Admin
```

By keeping the entire streaming experience within your branded academy environment, your community remains the vibrant center of student learning.

## Key Capabilities of the Rebuilt Video Stack

The modernized broadcast engine introduces several major functional advantages:

### 1. Sub-Second Latency
Because the platform utilizes direct WebRTC real-time media channels rather than traditional HTTP live streaming (HLS) buffering, latency is reduced to under 800 milliseconds. When an instructor asks a question on camera, students answer in the chat immediately, creating a genuine, conversational atmosphere.

### 2. Built-In Studio Controls
Instructors can share application windows, display slide presentations, invite guest co-hosts or students onto virtual stage seats, and manage audio feeds directly from their browser with no external broadcast encoders required.

### 3. Native Synchronized Chat and Reactions
The discussion feed is tied directly to the video playback engine, eliminating timeline drift and keeping comments aligned with the instructor current demonstration.

### 4. Zero-Click Recording Archiving
The moment you click "End Broadcast," the session recording begins automated server-side transcoding, publishing an HD replay to your designated community room or course module within minutes.

## Best Practices for Professional Live Streaming

To deliver polished live training sessions that keep your members coming back:

* **Invest in a Quality USB Microphone:** Clear audio is far more critical to adult learners than 4K video resolution. Use a dedicated dynamic microphone to minimize room echo.
* **Frame Your Background and Lighting:** Position a soft light source in front of your desk rather than behind you to ensure your face is well illuminated on screen.
* **Acknowledge Attendees by Name:** Take advantage of the sub-second latency to welcome students as they join and answer their questions in real time to foster connection.
