## Why So Many Online Courses Struggle

You are not alone if you have ever signed up for an online course and dropped out midway. Low engagement and low completion rates are the common challenges in the online learning industry.

The majority of online courses fail due to the following reasons:

- Firstly, not all courses are learner-centered, but content-based. The creators usually attempt to cover everything they are aware of and make the lessons too long, bombard them with information, and confuse.
- Second, they not structured the courses. Having no definite learning direction, the learners are not aware of what to pay attention to, and how one lesson relates to another.
- Third, the engagement is a secondary consideration. It is also easy, with all the best content in the world, to find watching video after video after interaction is boring.
- Lastly, most creators do not take the full advantage of the toolset that found on platforms such as Kajabi. There are powerful course-creating tools, which are not used properly unless an appropriate course strategy is used.

The result? Poor course completion rates, disappointed learners, and lost business opportunities.

## Modular Course Design: Learning in Small Steps

The most crucial aspect of a successful Kajabi course design is a modular structure.

We do not give long courses to the learners but rather subdivide content into small, focused modules. The modules have a single outcome, and thus the learners always know the outcome they are working towards.

Such a strategy promotes microlearning, which shows to enhance learning and memorization. Students can finish lessons during brief intervals, and thus it is easier to remain consistent despite their hectic schedule.

Modular design also:

- Reduces cognitive overload
- Quick wins help learners to gain confidence.
- Simplifies contents to update or increase.

As soon as learning becomes manageable, there are high chances of learners continuing and completing the course.

## Engagement Strategies: The Motivation of Learners

Interaction does not occur accidentally. It has to be purposefully structured.

We apply effective engagement strategies that enhance the motivation and involvement of learners, including:

- Thought-provoking reflection questions.
- Activities that are related to real-life scenarios.
- Progress reports indicate to the learners the distance covered.
- Online courses: Milestones or challenges in gamification.

These plans make the learners feel engaged and encouraged, as opposed to passive. Once the learners perceive progress and feel rewarded, chances are high that they remain committed to the course.
