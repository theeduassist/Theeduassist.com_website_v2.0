## **Best Practices for Thrilling, Selling Canva Development and Design**

To maximize impact:

1. **Apply Visual Design Principles**: Use contrast, alignment, repetition, proximity (C.R.A.P. from graphic design research). Canva’s auto-suggestions enforce this.
2. **Leverage Multimedia Learning**: Limit text, pair with images (Mayer-aligned).
3. **Ensure Accessibility and Inclusivity**: Follow UNESCO guidelines via Canva tools.
4. **Make It Interactive**: Add polls, quizzes, clickable elements for engagement.
5. **Brand and Personalize**: Custom kits for “selling” organizational culture.
6. **Measure and Iterate**: Export analytics; refine based on learner feedback.
