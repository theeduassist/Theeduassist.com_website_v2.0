## **FAQs**

**Are there different types of templates in Kajabi?**

Yes. Kajabi offers course templates, website templates, email templates, landing page templates, and membership/community templates.

**Can I customize a Kajabi course template after applying it?**

Yes. Most templates can be customized extensively using Kajabi’s visual editor.

**Can I import a template from another course?**

Yes. Kajabi allows creators to duplicate products and transfer layouts between courses.

**Do templates work with mobile and responsive design?**

Yes. Most modern Kajabi themes are fully mobile responsive.

**Are there limitations or downsides to using templates?**

Some free templates may look generic or have limited flexibility.

**Can I use a template for a new niche or language?**

Yes. Templates can be adapted for coaching, education, corporate training, memberships, and multilingual programs.

**How do I update or modify a template after launching?**

Templates can be edited post-launch through the theme customization settings.

**What’s the best way to choose a Kajabi template?**

Choose based on your audience, course structure, branding needs, conversion goals, and community strategy.
