# What is Admin Support?

Admin support involves routine tasks such as secretarial or management work, for example, handling calls or scheduling emails to keep your business running smoothly. For a Kajabi course creator, this support can mean having more time to focus on creating quality content instead of constantly going through emails, managing learner-related tasks, or keeping up with routine platform work.

Admin support can take some of that pressure away, allowing creators to spend more time developing their content and serving their learners. 

## Why does Admin Support matter for business:

Admin Support plays an important role in keeping a business organized, whether it operates online or in person, as Mikki Harris, Fractional COO and Founder & CEO at MOMENTUM Business Support Ltd, describes admin support as the ++[“backbone”](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/why-strong-admin-support-backbone-your-business-mikki-harris-p0kee/)++ of a business in her article on the role of administrative support.

The reason is simple: businesses have many routine tasks that need attention alongside their main work. Managing emails, scheduling, organizing information, responding to enquiries, and keeping day-to-day operations on track can take up a significant amount of time. For a Kajabi business, this can become even more noticeable as the business grows. A course creator may need to manage learners, update courses, respond to emails, organize offers, and keep different parts of their setup running while also creating new content. 



## How does Kajabi help you scale your business?



Kajabi helps you scale your business in multiple ways, as follows: 

### Bring Your Business Tools Together:

As your business grows, managing separate tools for your website, email, marketing, payments, and online courses can become difficult. Kajabi brings many of these features into one platform, so you can create and sell online courses, coaching programs, memberships, podcasts, and digital products without constantly switching between services.

### Automate Your Sales Process:

Kajabi's sales funnels, also known as pipelines, help guide potential customers from discovering your business to purchasing your products. You can connect landing pages, email campaigns, and offers to create a smoother sales process. This can reduce the amount of manual work involved in promoting your products and following up with potential customers. 

### Use Automation to Save Time:

You can also set up automations that respond to specific customer actions. For example, when someone purchases a course, you can automatically grant them access and send a follow-up email. You can also trigger emails when someone signs up for a free resource or completes a lesson. These automations help you manage routine tasks while keeping customers informed throughout their experience. 

### Track Your Business Performance:

Growing a business also means understanding what is working and what needs improvement. Kajabi's analytics tools can help you review your business performance and identify useful trends in areas such as revenue, sales, and customer engagement. By regularly reviewing this information, you can make more informed decisions about your products, marketing, and next steps. 

### Build Your Brand in One Place:

As your audience grows, having a consistent brand identity becomes increasingly important. Kajabi allows you to create a website, use a custom domain, and deliver your products under your own brand. This helps you build a recognizable online presence and gives customers a more consistent experience as they discover, purchase, and access your content. 

## What are the features of Admin Support?

Admin support helps keep daily business tasks organized, from managing emails and customer records to updating course materials and schedules. As your Kajabi business grows, this support can save time and help you focus on creating products and reaching more customers.

## Should you get Admin Support for your Kajabi business?

If managing daily tasks is taking time away from creating content and growing your business, admin support may be worth considering. It can help you stay organized and handle routine responsibilities as your workload increases. However, if you're just starting out, you may be able to manage these tasks yourself until your business grows enough to need extra help.

## Final thoughts

Growing a Kajabi business takes time, planning, and the right support. Admin support can help you manage daily tasks more efficiently, giving you more time to focus on your products, customers, and long-term business goals.
