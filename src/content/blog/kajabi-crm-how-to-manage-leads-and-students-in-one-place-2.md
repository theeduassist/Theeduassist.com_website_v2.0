# Kajabi Email Broadcast vs. Sequence: Which Should You Use?

If you’ve been inside your Kajabi dashboard staring at the Email section, you’ve probably asked yourself: **Should I send a broadcast or set up a sequence?**

It’s one of the most common questions course creators, coaches, and online educators face. Choosing the wrong option can lead to poorly timed emails, inconsistent communication, and lower engagement.

The good news is that once you understand how each tool works and why it exists, the decision becomes much easier.

This guide explains the difference between a **Kajabi email broadcast and an email sequence** in simple terms, with practical examples for educators, coaches, course creators, and online businesses.

## What Is a Kajabi Email Broadcast?

A **Kajabi email broadcast** is a one-time email you send to a specific group of subscribers immediately or at a scheduled time. Think of it like a company-wide announcement.

Broadcasts are best for time-sensitive or moment-specific communication. Something is happening, and you want your audience to know about it.

### Common uses for Kajabi broadcasts

You can use a broadcast for:

- Announcing a new course or product
- Promoting a limited-time discount
- Sharing a new blog post or YouTube video
- Notifying students about a live webinar or Q&A
- Sending a weekly newsletter
- Sharing community updates
- Announcing an important change or event

Broadcasts do not repeat automatically. Once the email is sent, it is finished. Someone who joins your list tomorrow will not automatically receive the broadcast you sent today.

If you want new subscribers to receive similar information automatically, you can turn the concept into an email sequence.

## What Is a Kajabi Email Sequence?

A **Kajabi email sequence** is a series of pre-written emails that are delivered automatically according to a schedule or trigger.

For example, a subscriber might receive the first email immediately after signing up, another email two days later, and another several days after that.

Sequences are particularly useful when you're building a structured customer or learner journey. If you're still setting up your Kajabi account, our [Kajabi Account Setup Guide for Beginners](https://www.theeduassist.com/blog/kajabi-account-setup-guide/) can help you understand the basic platform setup before creating your email system.

### Common uses for Kajabi sequences

Sequences can be used for:

- Welcome emails for new subscribers
- Lead magnet delivery
- Course onboarding
- Post-purchase follow-ups
- Lead nurturing
- Free email courses
- Membership onboarding
- Re-engagement campaigns

The biggest advantage of a sequence is consistency. You create the emails once, and they can continue working automatically for people who enter the sequence later.

## Broadcast vs. Sequence: The Core Difference

The easiest way to understand the difference is to look at **timing, purpose, audience, personalization, and effort**.


| Feature | Kajabi Broadcast | Kajabi Sequence |
| ---------- | ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Timing | Sent at a specific time | Triggered and scheduled automatically |
| Purpose | Announcements, updates, promotions | Nurturing, onboarding, education |
| Audience | Existing subscribers or selected segments | People who enter the sequence |
| Automation | Mostly manual | Automated |
| Setup | Quick to create | More setup required |
| Repetition | One-time | Multiple emails |
| Best for | Time-sensitive communication | Ongoing customer journeys |


Neither tool is automatically better than the other. A strong Kajabi email strategy usually uses **both broadcasts and sequences together**.

## When to Use a Kajabi Email Broadcast

### 1. You Have Time-Sensitive News

If something is happening right now, a broadcast is usually the better choice.

For example:

- Your sale ends tonight
- A webinar starts tomorrow
- You published a new lesson
- A new product is available
- You have an important community announcement

These situations depend on a specific date or event, so sending a one-time broadcast makes sense.

### 2. You’re Running a Course Launch

Kajabi broadcasts can be useful during a course launch because launch communication often depends on specific dates.

You might send:

- A launch announcement
- A valuable educational email
- An objection-handling email
- A student success story
- A deadline reminder

Each email can be scheduled around the launch calendar.

If you are also working on your course website and funnel, you may want to read our guide on [how to create a course on Kajabi](https://www.theeduassist.com/blog/how-to-create-a-course-on-kajabi-beginners-guide/) for additional setup guidance.

### 3. You Want to Stay Connected With Your Existing Audience

Broadcasts are also useful for regular newsletters, weekly tips, new content, and community updates.

For example, you could send a monthly email highlighting:

- New lessons
- New blog posts
- Upcoming events
- New resources
- Student success stories

This keeps your existing audience informed without putting everyone into an automated sequence.

## When to Use a Kajabi Email Sequence

### 1. You Have a Lead Magnet or Opt-In

When someone downloads a free checklist, guide, template, or other resource, a welcome sequence can automatically introduce them to your brand.

A simple sequence might include:

**Day 0:** Welcome and resource delivery  
**Day 2:** Helpful tip related to the resource  
**Day 4:** Educational content  
**Day 6:** Your story or expertise  
**Day 8:** Introduction to a relevant paid offer

The exact timing depends on your audience and offer, but the important point is that the journey happens automatically.

### 2. After Someone Buys a Course or Joins a Membership

Post-purchase sequences can help students understand what to do next.

For example:

**Day 0:** Welcome and access instructions  
**Day 2:** Where to start + quick-win lesson  
**Day 5:** Community invitation and support resources  
**Day 10:** Progress check-in and encouragement

This type of onboarding can be especially useful if you have spent time building a structured course experience.

If you're comparing Kajabi with other learning platforms, you can also explore [LearnWorlds vs. Kajabi](https://www.theeduassist.com/blog/learnworlds-vs-kajabi/) to understand how the platforms differ.

### 3. You’re Nurturing Cold Leads

Not every subscriber is ready to purchase immediately.

A nurture sequence can provide useful information over several days or weeks. Instead of repeatedly asking for a sale, you can use the sequence to answer common questions, demonstrate expertise, and explain how your product or course works.

This is where Kajabi's automation features become particularly useful. For a deeper look, see our guide to [Kajabi automation workflows](https://www.theeduassist.com/blog/kajabi-automation-workflows-how-to-scale-smarter-and-sell-more-courses-in-2026/).

### 4. You Want to Deliver a Free Email Course

A free email course can also be delivered through a sequence.

For example, you could create a seven-day mini-course:

- Day 1: Introduction
- Day 2: Basic concept
- Day 3: Practical exercise
- Day 4: Common mistakes
- Day 5: Advanced tip
- Day 6: Case study
- Day 7: Next steps

Every new subscriber can start the same journey regardless of when they join your list.

## Combining Broadcasts and Sequences

The most practical approach is not choosing between broadcasts and sequences. Instead, use each one for the job it handles best.

Here is a simple example.

Someone discovers your content through search or social media and signs up for a free checklist.

**Sequence:**  
Welcome email → free resource → educational emails → soft introduction to your paid offer

If they don't purchase immediately, your regular **broadcasts** can continue the relationship.

You might send:

- Weekly educational tips
- New blog posts
- Product updates
- Webinar invitations
- Limited-time promotions

When you launch a new product, you can then use broadcasts for the time-sensitive launch communication.

This creates a simple system:

**Sequences handle the automated journey. Broadcasts handle ongoing communication and time-sensitive events.**

## Kajabi Email Segmentation

Sending every email to your entire list is not always the best approach.

Where appropriate, segment your audience based on factors such as:

- Products purchased
- Offers
- Tags
- Subscriber behavior
- Course enrollment
- Customer status

For example, someone who has already purchased your course may not need an email promoting the same introductory offer.

## Quick Tips for Better Kajabi Email Engagement

### Segment Your Broadcasts

Don't automatically send every email to your entire database. Use relevant audience segments when appropriate so subscribers receive content that matches their interests or relationship with your business.

### Write Natural Subject Lines

Your subject line should make the reader understand why the email is worth opening.

Avoid relying too heavily on excessive capitalization, emojis, or artificial urgency.

A simple subject such as **“A quick update about your course”** can be more natural than a subject line packed with promotional language.

### Keep Sequences Focused

Each sequence email should have a clear purpose.

One email might deliver a lesson. Another might answer a common question. Another might encourage the learner to take the next step.

Avoid putting too many unrelated ideas into one email.

### Test Before Going Live

Before activating a sequence, send test emails to yourself.

Check:

- Links
- Formatting
- Images
- Personalization
- Timing
- Calls to action
- Mobile readability

A small testing process can prevent avoidable mistakes.

### Track Your Results

Pay attention to metrics such as:

- Open rate
- Click rate
- Unsubscribe rate
- Conversion rate
- Engagement over time

If one email consistently performs differently from the others, review its subject line, content, timing, and call to action.

## FAQs

### Can I convert a broadcast into a sequence email?

You can repurpose the content of a broadcast for a sequence email. However, you should review the message first because a broadcast may contain date-specific information that would not make sense in an automated sequence.

### How many emails should a welcome sequence have?

A practical starting point is **3–5 emails over approximately 7–10 days**. You can adjust the number and timing based on your audience and results.

A basic welcome sequence can introduce your brand, deliver the promised resource, provide useful information, and introduce the next relevant step.

### Does Kajabi support conditional logic in sequences?

Kajabi provides automation capabilities that can help you create different actions based on subscriber activity. For more complex workflows, review the platform's current automation capabilities and structure your tags, offers, and sequences carefully.

### What is a good open rate for Kajabi emails?

There is no single open-rate benchmark that applies to every Kajabi business. Results can vary based on audience quality, industry, list age, subject lines, email frequency, and whether the email is automated or broadcast.

Rather than focusing on one universal number, compare your own campaigns over time.

### Should I use Kajabi Email or a separate tool like ConvertKit?

For many Kajabi users, the built-in email features can be sufficient, particularly when you want your course, contacts, offers, and email workflows in one platform.

As your business becomes more complex, you can compare Kajabi with dedicated email marketing platforms and choose based on the features your workflow actually requires.

If you're still evaluating Kajabi's usability, you can also read [Is Kajabi Easy to Use?](https://www.theeduassist.com/blog/is-kajabi-easy-to-use/) for a beginner-focused overview.

### Can sequences and broadcasts be sent to the same subscriber?

Yes. A subscriber can receive both automated sequence emails and manual broadcasts.

However, pay attention to email frequency. Sending too many messages within a short period can create a poor subscriber experience and increase unsubscribes.

## Final Thoughts

Kajabi gives course creators two useful email tools, but they serve different purposes.

**Broadcasts** are ideal for announcements, launches, newsletters, promotions, and other time-sensitive communication.

**Sequences** are better suited to automated journeys such as welcome campaigns, lead nurturing, course onboarding, and educational email courses.

The most effective setup is often a combination of both.

Use sequences to automate the parts of your customer journey that repeat, and use broadcasts when you need to communicate something relevant to your audience at a specific moment.
