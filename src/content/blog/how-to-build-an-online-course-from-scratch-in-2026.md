> Creating an online course has never been more accessible — but without a clear plan, it's easy to get overwhelmed. Here's how to do it right.
>
> **1. Validate Your Idea First**  
> Before recording a single video, make sure people actually want your course. Post about the topic on social media, run a quick survey, or pre-sell to a small audience. If nobody's interested before it's built, they won't be after either.
>
> **2. Define Your Learning Outcomes**  
> What will your students be able to do after completing your course? Write 3-5 clear outcomes before you build anything. These become your roadmap.
>
> **3. Outline Your Curriculum**  
> Break your course into modules and lessons. A good rule of thumb: each lesson should cover one concept and take no more than 10-15 minutes to complete.
>
> **4. Choose Your Platform**  
> Kajabi, Teachable, and Thinkific are popular choices. Each has different strengths — Kajabi is all-in-one, Teachable is beginner-friendly, Thinkific has a generous free plan.
>
> **5. Record and Edit Your Content**  
> You don't need a professional studio. Good lighting, a decent microphone, and a quiet room are enough to start. Tools like Loom or OBS are free and easy to use.
>
> **6. Launch and Iterate**  
> Your first version doesn't need to be perfect — it needs to exist. Launch to a small group, collect feedback, and improve from there.
>
> **Final Thought**  
> The best online course is the one that actually gets finished and published. Start simple, stay consistent, and refine as you grow.
