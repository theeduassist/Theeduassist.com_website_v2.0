## **What Is Kajabi Affiliate Marketing Setup?**

Simply put, the Kajabi affiliate marketing setup is the process of switching on Kajabi’s built-in affiliate tools.

After that, other people can sell your products for you. They earn a fee. You earn revenue. It is a win for both sides.

Here is how it works:

* First, you create an offer inside Kajabi.
* Then, you pick a commission rate.
* Next, Kajabi gives each affiliate their own link.
* When someone buys through that link, the affiliate earns money.
* Finally, you only pay when a sale is made.
As a result, this costs you nothing until it works. That is why so many Kajabi creators love this model.


### **Why Kajabi Works So Well for Affiliates**

Unlike other tools, Kajabi has everything built in. So, you do not need extra software.

* Live dashboard: see clicks, sales, and earnings in real time.
* Unique links: each partner gets their own tracking URL.
* Auto payouts: Kajabi handles payments for you.
* Product sync: works with all your courses and offers.
In fact, research shows that affiliate marketing now drives a massive 16% of all online sales. Because of this, it has easily become one of the single best growth tools available for course creators today.


## **How to Set Up Affiliate Program in Kajabi (5 Steps)**

Good news: setup takes under 30 minutes. Just follow these five steps.


### **Step 1: Turn On Affiliate Tracking**

* Log into your Kajabi account.
* Then, click Settings in the left menu.
* Next, go to the Affiliates tab.
* Finally, switch the system to ON.

### **Step 2: Build Your Affiliate Offer**

Now, go to Sales. Then click Offers. Pick the product you want to promote. After that, turn on affiliate tracking for that offer.


### **Step 3: Choose Your Commission Rate**

Next, set a rate for your offer. For instance, you can add recurring commissions for subscriptions. As a rule, 25% to 40% works best because it quickly attracts strong partners.


### **Step 4: Send Out Affiliate Links**

Fortunately, Kajabi creates a unique link for each affiliate. So, you can just send that link to your partners. Additionally, all clicks and sales are tracked live.


### **Step 5: Test Before You Go Live**

Before you invite anyone, click your own link first. Then check your dashboard. Make sure the click shows up. This simple check saves a lot of confusion later.

## **How to Find Your First Kajabi Affiliates**

This is where most beginners get stuck. However, it does not have to be hard. You just need to know where to look.


### **Best Places to Find Affiliates**

Start close to home. Then, branch out to these spots:

* Your email list: your best students make the best affiliates. Ask them first.
* Facebook Groups: look for active creators in your niche.
* LinkedIn: Search for educators in your topic. Send a short note.
* YouTube: find creators with 1,000 to 50,000 followers in your space.
* Reddit: try subreddits like r/OnlineCourses to find motivated people.

### **A Simple Outreach Formula**

When you reach out, keep it brief. Here is what works:

* Praise one thing about their content.
* Then, explain your offer in one line.
* Next, state your commission rate.
* Finally, add a link to sign up.
Also, try an early bonus. For example, give the first 10 affiliates an extra 5% for 90 days. This builds urgency. It also rewards your top early partners.

## **Common Kajabi Affiliate Marketing Mistakes to Avoid**

Even smart creators make these errors. So, learn from them now:

* **Rate too low:** anything under 20% drives affiliates to other programs.
* **No welcome email: **always send clear setup instructions on day one**.**
* **Ignoring data: **check your dashboard each week and reward top earners.
* **Wrong fit:** only recruit affiliates whose audience matches your students.

## **FAQs About Kajabi Affiliate Marketing Setup**


### **How do I get affiliates for Kajabi?**

To begin, you should start with your own community. For example, you can ask your current students and email subscribers first. After that, you can easily reach out to other niche creators on LinkedIn or Instagram to grow your network even further.


### **What is a good affiliate commission rate?**

For digital courses, offering a rate between 25% and 40% is a solid start. On the other hand, recurring programs work best at 15% to 30% per month because this model gives partners a steady, long-term payout.


### **Can I make affiliate links for my Kajabi course?**

Yes. Kajabi builds unique links for each offer. Simply share them from your dashboard.


### **Is Kajabi good for affiliate beginners?**

Absolutely. The system is built in. Moreover, it is simple to use and needs no coding at all.
