## **Step 1: Define Your Project Scope**

Before you search for a Kajabi website designer, first be clear about your needs. Are you building a full site or only updating a few pages?


### **Identify Your Needs**

* **Full Website:** This includes homepage, library, store, and blog pages.
* **Funnels Only:** You may only need landing pages and checkout pages.
* **Special Layouts:** Some coaches need Kajabi website design for coaches with booking features.
When you define your needs early, you can talk more clearly with designers. In addition, you get better price estimates and more accurate time estimates. Therefore, the whole process becomes smoother and more efficient

You do not always need to start from zero. In fact, using ready-made designs can save time and money.


### **Using Existing Themes**

Many agencies offer premium Kajabi website design templates for your brand.

These templates are better than default platform designs.

When you hire experts for Kajabi website design services, they can adjust these templates easily. Then they add your colors, fonts, and images. As a result, you get a custom look at lower cost.


## **Step 3: Check Real Examples for Ideas**

Before choosing a designer, it helps to look at real websites. Studying live Kajabi website examples shows what is possible.

* Check if pages load fast.
* Look at clear call-to-action buttons.
* Review how smooth the checkout process is.
A good agency offering Kajabi website design services will show real live websites. If you find a design you like, that may be the right partner.


## **Step 4: Check Technical Skills**

Good design is not enough. Your website must also work properly. Therefore, technical skill is very important.


### **Design and Development Balance**

A designer focuses on how the site looks. A Kajabi website developer focuses on how it works.

They make sure email automation, payments, and funnels work well.

This is important for memberships and multiple product setups.

A team that offers full Kajabi website design services gives you both design and technical support. In addition, they manage all tasks in a simple and clear way. As a result, you get a full solution with no confusion and no extra effort needed.


## **Step 5: Talk and Choose Your Designer**

After you shortlist experts, book short calls with them. Use these calls to understand how they work.

* Can you show past Kajabi website design work?
* Do your Kajabi website design services include SEO and mobile setup?

A good expert will listen carefully and give you a clear plan.
