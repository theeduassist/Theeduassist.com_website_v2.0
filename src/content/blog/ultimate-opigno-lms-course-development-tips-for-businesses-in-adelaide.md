## Key Tips for Effective Opigno LMS Course Development

### 1. Understand Your Learners

- Conduct surveys and interviews to identify skill gaps.
- Segment learners by roles, departments, or experience level.
- Create personalized learning experiences to boost engagement.

### 2. Design Engaging Content

- Use a mix of videos, quizzes, interactive modules, and gamification.
- Keep lessons concise to maintain attention.
- Include real-world scenarios and case studies relevant to Adelaide businesses.

### 3. Structure Courses Strategically

- Organize content into modules or units for easy navigation.
- Include prerequisites and recommended paths to guide learners.
- Use clear learning objectives for each module.

### 4. Integrate Assessments Effectively

- Include formative assessments after each module.
- Use quizzes, assignments, and surveys to evaluate understanding.
- Provide immediate feedback to reinforce learning.

### 5. Monitor and Improve

- Track learner engagement and completion rates.
- Gather feedback through surveys and course analytics.
- Regularly update content to keep it relevant and impactful.

## Step-by-Step Opigno LMS Course Development Checklist

Follow this proven framework to create high-impact courses:

### 1. Organisation & Planning Phase

- Identify business-specific needs (e.g., WHS refreshers, customer service for tourism, or compliance for defence contractors).
- Understand your audience — segment learners by role, location, or skill level using Opigno's user and group management tools.
- Define clear, measurable learning objectives using Bloom's Taxonomy.
- Map the full learner journey, including prerequisites and integration with HR systems via API or SSO.

### 2. Course Design & Development Phase

- Build logical structure — Use courses as containers, lessons for topics, and activities as individual interactions. Choose guided (sequential) or free navigation as needed.
- Leverage H5P for interactivity — Embed interactive videos, quizzes, drag-and-drop exercises, timelines, and branching scenarios directly in the platform.
- Incorporate multimedia (videos, PDFs, presentations) and make content reusable across departments.
- Add gamification elements such as points, badges, and leaderboards.
- Enable adaptive learning paths based on performance and support blended learning with instructor-led sessions.
- Ensure WCAG accessibility and mobile optimisation for all devices.

### 3. Publishing, Testing & Iteration Phase

- Test thoroughly across devices and gather pilot feedback from Adelaide employees.
- Set smart permissions and visibility rules.
- Launch using built-in notifications and calendar tools.
- Track results with detailed analytics and xAPI support. Review and update courses regularly to reflect changing SA regulations and industry standards.
