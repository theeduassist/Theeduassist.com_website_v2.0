## The Strategic Difference: Relative Drip vs Calendar Date Drip

Understanding when to deploy relative drip versus calendar date drip is essential for effective curriculum delivery:

```
[Relative Day Drip (Best for Evergreen Self-Paced Courses)]
Student A Enrolls on Oct 1 ──> Module 1 (Immediate) ──> Module 2 on Oct 8 ──> Module 3 on Oct 15
Student B Enrolls on Oct 5 ──> Module 1 (Immediate) ──> Module 2 on Oct 12 ──> Module 3 on Oct 19
* Outcome: Students are at completely different stages; community discussion cannot sync.

[Specific Calendar Date Drip (Best for Cohorts, Masterminds, Corporate Academies)]
All Enrolled Students (Enrolled anytime before kickoff)
   └── Oct 12 at 9:00 AM: Module 1 Unlocks Simultaneously for Everyone
   └── Oct 19 at 9:00 AM: Module 2 Unlocks Simultaneously for Everyone
   └── Oct 26 at 9:00 AM: Module 3 Unlocks Simultaneously for Everyone
* Outcome: Shared momentum, focused peer discussions, and synchronized live Q&A sessions.
```

By scheduling lesson unlocks to concrete calendar dates, you create collective anticipation. Every member of the cohort encounters the same assignments, dilemmas, and exercises concurrently.

## Practical Setup: Configuring Calendar Drip in the Product Outline

Setting up specific calendar releases inside the modernized Kajabi course outline requires just a few intuitive configuration steps:

### Step 1: Structure Your Categories and Modules
Open **Products > Courses** and select your curriculum. Group your lessons into logical weekly categories (e.g., `Week 1: Market Analysis`, `Week 2: Financial Modeling`).

### Step 2: Set Category Release Rules
Hover over the category status indicator (which defaults to Draft or Published) and select **Drip**. 

### Step 3: Choose "Specific Date" and Set Release Timing
In the updated modal dialog, select **By Specific Date**. Use the calendar date picker to select your designated unlock date and specify your preferred hour (such as `09:00 AM EST`).

### Step 4: Enable Automated Student Notification
Check the option to send an automated system email to enrolled students the instant the category unlocks. This alerts students that new training material is waiting inside their student dashboard.

## Managing Late Enrollments and Archive Policies

A common operational challenge in cohort training is handling students who register 48 hours after the formal launch kickoff:

* **Retroactive Module Access:** With Kajabi specific date drip, any module whose scheduled release date has already passed unlocks immediately upon checkout. A student who joins on Wednesday during Week 1 gets immediate access to Week 1 content without admin intervention.
* **Preserving Post-Cohort Archives:** Once the live cohort concludes, creators can simply switch the category status from Drip to Published. This leaves all historical recordings and assets permanently accessible for lifelong review.
