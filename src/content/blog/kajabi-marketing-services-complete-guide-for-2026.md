If you use Kajabi to sell online courses, coaching programs, memberships, digital products, or other online learning products, having the platform is only the beginning. You also need a clear marketing strategy to attract the right audience, generate leads, nurture prospects, and turn them into customers.

**Kajabi marketing services** help businesses plan, build, and improve their marketing activities using Kajabi. These services can include email marketing, sales funnels, landing pages, automation, audience segmentation, SEO, content marketing, product launches, and analytics.

Kajabi already provides many of these tools within one platform. The real challenge is knowing how to connect them into a marketing system that fits your business.

At **TheEduAssist**, we focus on practical digital learning and marketing strategies that help course creators, educators, coaches, and online businesses make better use of platforms such as Kajabi.

This guide explains what Kajabi marketing services include, when you may need them, how they work, and what to consider before hiring a Kajabi marketing specialist.

## What Are Kajabi Marketing Services?

Kajabi marketing services are professional services designed to help businesses use Kajabi's marketing features more effectively.

Depending on your goals, a Kajabi marketing specialist may help with:

- Kajabi marketing strategy
- Email marketing
- Sales funnel setup
- Landing page optimization
- Marketing automation
- Audience segmentation
- Lead generation
- SEO and content marketing
- Product launches
- Webinar campaigns
- Affiliate marketing
- Conversion optimization
- Marketing analytics

You do not necessarily need every service.

For example, a new course creator may need a simple lead-generation funnel and welcome email sequence. An established coaching business may need advanced automation, SEO, email segmentation, and ongoing funnel optimization.

The right approach depends on your audience, offer, marketing goals, and existing system.

## What Does a Kajabi Marketing Specialist Do?

A Kajabi marketing specialist connects your marketing strategy with Kajabi's available tools.

The work can include both planning and implementation.

A typical project may involve:

1. Understanding the target audience.
2. Reviewing the existing products and offers.
3. Mapping the customer journey.
4. Identifying marketing gaps.
5. Planning lead-generation campaigns.
6. Creating landing pages.
7. Building email campaigns.
8. Setting up automation.
9. Organizing contacts and segments.
10. Reviewing campaign performance.

A technically correct Kajabi funnel can still perform poorly if the offer is unclear or the campaign targets the wrong audience.

That is why strategy should come before building pages and automations.

## Kajabi Marketing Services You May Need

### 1. Kajabi Marketing Strategy

A marketing strategy gives your campaigns a clear direction.

Before building a funnel or email sequence, you should understand:

- Who your ideal customer is
- What problem they want to solve
- What makes your offer useful
- Which marketing channels you will use
- What action you want visitors to take
- How leads will be nurtured
- How success will be measured

For example, a business selling a $30 digital product may use a simple:

**Blog → Lead Magnet → Email Sequence → Product Offer**

A coaching business may need:

**SEO/Social Media → Lead Magnet → Email Nurturing → Webinar → Consultation → Coaching Program**

Both can use Kajabi, but their marketing systems are different.

## 2. Kajabi Email Marketing

Email marketing is one of the most important parts of a Kajabi marketing strategy.

Kajabi supports email broadcasts and email sequences that can be used for different stages of the customer journey.

For a deeper look at Kajabi email marketing, see TheEduAssist's guide to **[Kajabi email campaigns](https://theeduassist.com/blog/kajabi-emails-campaigns/)**.

### Email Broadcasts

Broadcasts are useful for one-time communications such as:

- Newsletters
- Product announcements
- Promotional campaigns
- Launch updates
- Important business updates

### Email Sequences

Sequences are useful when you want emails to be sent automatically over time.

Examples include:

- Welcome sequences
- Lead-nurturing campaigns
- Course onboarding
- Product launch sequences
- Sales sequences
- Re-engagement campaigns

For example:

**Visitor downloads a free guide → Receives welcome email → Gets educational emails → Receives product offer**

This allows the business to continue communicating with leads without manually sending every message.

## 3. Kajabi Sales Funnel Setup

A sales funnel guides a potential customer from their first interaction with your business toward a specific action.

A simple Kajabi funnel may look like:

**Landing Page → Lead Capture → Email Sequence → Sales Page → Checkout**

A more advanced funnel might be:

**Blog → Lead Magnet → Email Nurturing → Webinar → Sales Sequence → Offer**

Kajabi marketing services may include planning and building:

- Lead-generation funnels
- Sales funnels
- Webinar funnels
- Product-launch funnels
- Evergreen funnels
- Lead-magnet funnels
- Course enrollment funnels

A good funnel does not have to be complicated.

The goal is to make the next step clear and relevant to the customer.

## 4. Kajabi Landing Page Optimization

Landing pages play an important role in lead generation and sales.

A Kajabi landing page should quickly answer:

**What is this?**

**Who is it for?**

**What problem does it solve?**

**What should I do next?**

Landing page optimization can include:

- Headline improvements
- Clearer messaging
- Stronger calls to action
- Better page structure
- Lead-capture optimization
- Mobile usability
- Offer positioning
- Conversion-focused copy

For example, a free-course landing page should make the value of the free course obvious instead of forcing visitors to read a long explanation before taking action.

If you're also working on the visual side of your Kajabi website, TheEduAssist's guide to **[Kajabi website templates](https://www.theeduassist.com/blog/best-kajabi-website-templates/)** can provide additional context.

## 5. Kajabi Marketing Automation

Marketing automation helps reduce repetitive tasks and keep customer journeys organized.

Examples include:

**When someone submits a form → Add them to a welcome sequence**

**When someone purchases a course → Grant access and send onboarding emails**

**When someone registers for a webinar → Send confirmation and reminder emails**

**When someone completes an action → Apply a relevant tag**

Automation can save time, but it should be used carefully.

Adding more automation does not automatically create better marketing. Every workflow should have a clear purpose.

TheEduAssist also covers **[Kajabi automation workflows](https://www.theeduassist.com/blog/kajabi-automation-workflows-how-to-scale-smarter-and-sell-more-courses-in-2026/)** in more detail, including lead nurturing, onboarding, re-engagement, and other practical workflows.

Kajabi's official documentation explains its automation system using **When, Then, and Only if** logic.

## 6. Audience Segmentation

As your email list grows, sending the same message to everyone becomes less useful.

Audience segmentation allows you to communicate with people based on their interests, behavior, products, or relationship with your business.

Possible segments include:

- New subscribers
- Existing customers
- Course students
- Webinar registrants
- Lead-magnet subscribers
- Previous customers
- Highly engaged subscribers
- Inactive subscribers

For example, someone who has already purchased your beginner course may be interested in an advanced course rather than another beginner-level promotion.

Better segmentation can make your marketing communication more relevant.

## 11. Kajabi Analytics and Reporting

Marketing decisions should be based on meaningful data rather than traffic numbers alone.

Depending on your campaign, useful metrics may include:


| Metric | What It Tells You |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Website traffic | How many people reach your website |
| Leads | How many prospects enter your funnel |
| Email engagement | Whether subscribers interact with emails |
| Conversion rate | How effectively visitors become customers |
| Sales | Revenue generated by campaigns |
| Subscriber growth | Whether your audience is expanding |
| Customer source | Where customers are discovering your business |
| Retention | Whether customers continue using recurring products |


The most useful metrics depend on the objective.

For example, an SEO campaign may focus on organic traffic and leads, while a product launch may focus more heavily on registrations, conversions, and revenue.

Kajabi also provides funnel statistics that can show metrics such as conversion rate, revenue, views, conversions, sales, and email sequence activity.

## Kajabi Marketing Services vs. DIY Marketing

Kajabi is designed to allow businesses to manage many marketing activities themselves.

However, doing everything internally requires time and knowledge.


| DIY Marketing | Professional Kajabi Support |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Lower external cost | Requires a service budget |
| Full control | Specialist handles implementation |
| Requires learning time | Can reduce setup time |
| Suitable for simple campaigns | Useful for complex campaigns |
| You manage optimization | Specialist can assist with optimization |
| Good when you have time | Useful when marketing becomes a bottleneck |


You do not have to outsource everything.

For example, you could manage your weekly newsletter internally while hiring a Kajabi specialist to build a new sales funnel or automate your customer onboarding.

## When Should You Hire a Kajabi Marketing Specialist?

Professional support may be useful if:

- You have Kajabi but are unsure how to structure your funnel.
- Your website receives traffic but generates few leads.
- Your email list is growing but sales are not keeping pace.
- Your automations are becoming difficult to manage.
- You are preparing for a product launch.
- You need help with Kajabi SEO.
- You want to improve an existing funnel.
- You do not have enough time to manage campaigns.
- You need a structured email marketing strategy.
- You want ongoing marketing analysis.

The important thing is to identify the actual bottleneck first.

If your problem is poor organic visibility, hiring someone only to redesign your landing page may not solve the main issue.

## How to Choose Kajabi Marketing Services

### 1. Identify Your Main Problem

Start with the problem rather than the service.

Do you need:

- More organic traffic?
- More leads?
- Better email nurturing?
- A new sales funnel?
- Better automation?
- Launch support?
- Higher conversion rates?

### 2. Check Relevant Experience

Look for experience that matches your business model.

Someone who mainly builds simple landing pages may not be the right choice for a complex launch funnel.

### 3. Understand What Is Included

Before starting, clarify whether the service includes:

- Strategy
- Copywriting
- Design
- Kajabi implementation
- Automation
- Testing
- Analytics
- Revisions
- Ongoing support

### 4. Ask How Results Will Be Measured

The provider should be able to explain which metrics will be monitored.

### 5. Understand the Process

Ask about:

- Project timeline
- Communication
- Approvals
- Reporting
- Access requirements
- Post-launch support

### 6. Avoid Guaranteed Results

Marketing performance depends on many factors, including the offer, audience, competition, traffic sources, messaging, and market conditions.

A good provider should explain the strategy and measurement process rather than promise a guaranteed amount of traffic or revenue.

## Common Kajabi Marketing Mistakes

### Using Every Kajabi Feature

More features do not automatically mean better marketing.

Start with the customer journey and use features only when they solve a specific problem.

### Building Funnels Before Understanding Your Audience

A well-designed funnel cannot compensate for unclear positioning or the wrong target audience.

### Sending the Same Email to Everyone

As your list grows, segmentation can make your communication more relevant.

### Ignoring SEO

Depending entirely on social media or paid advertising can limit opportunities to build long-term organic visibility.

### Over-Automating

Automation should save time without making the customer experience feel irrelevant or impersonal.

### Measuring Only Traffic

More traffic is not necessarily useful if those visitors are unlikely to become customers.

Track metrics that connect to your actual business objective.

### Never Updating Existing Campaigns

Your audience, offer, competitors, and market can change.

Review older campaigns and improve them when the data shows an opportunity.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are Kajabi marketing services?

Kajabi marketing services help businesses plan, build, manage, and optimize marketing activities using Kajabi. These services can include email marketing, funnels, automation, SEO, content marketing, product launches, and analytics.

### Does Kajabi include marketing tools?

Yes. Kajabi provides marketing functionality including email campaigns, funnels, landing pages, automations, contact management, segmentation, and analytics.

### Can Kajabi be used for email marketing?

Yes. Kajabi supports email broadcasts and email sequences. Broadcasts are useful for individual campaigns, while sequences can be used for automated email communication.

### Can Kajabi automate marketing?

Yes. Kajabi provides automation tools that can connect actions and triggers across different parts of a customer's journey.

### Do I need a Kajabi marketing agency?

Not necessarily. Businesses with simple marketing systems can often manage Kajabi themselves. Professional support can become useful when funnels, automation, SEO, launches, or ongoing optimization become time-consuming.

### How much do Kajabi marketing services cost?

There is no fixed price. The cost depends on the scope of work, complexity, provider experience, and whether you need a one-time project or ongoing support.

### Is Kajabi good for SEO?

Kajabi can be used as part of an SEO strategy, but search performance depends on many factors beyond the platform, including content quality, search intent, technical SEO, authority, competition, and overall website usefulness.

### What Kajabi marketing service should I outsource first?

Start with the area creating the biggest bottleneck. Depending on the business, this could be SEO, funnel development, email automation, content strategy, or conversion optimization.
