They want this experience to help them understand learning better and keep them interested in the online learning they are doing. Online learning should be fun and interesting, for people who are learning online. This is where things like augmented reality and virtual reality are making a difference in online education.

By just sitting and watching classes students can now work with digital things, visit virtual places and try out real life skills in a safe and fun way. Augmented reality and virtual reality in education are really changing how people learn. Students can interact with content, go to virtual worlds and practise what they learn in a secure and engaging way.

## **Can AR and VR enhance employee training programs? How do they work?**

AR and VR are really becoming useful tools for making workplace training and professional development a lot better. People are using augmented reality and virtual reality more and more to help them learn things at work.

Many organizations struggle with the limitations of traditional training approaches. Classroom sessions and instructional videos often fall short when it comes to preparing workers for the situations they will actually encounter on the job.

Immersive training gives employees a chance to practice and develop skills in realistic environments before they ever need to apply those skills in a real work setting.

Common applications include:

**Standard Safety Training**

Employees can practise emergency procedures without any actual risk.

**Equipment Operation**

Workers can practice operating machinery on simulators before they ever touch the real equipment.

**Customer Service Training**

Employees can use situations to get better at talking to people and solving problems.

**Leadership Growth**

Managers can try out simulations that help them make decisions and manage their teams.

These kinds of experiences help people learn and feel more confident, without taking risks, which also helps keep the cost of training from getting too high. Employees can use situations to improve their communication skills and problem-solving skills.
