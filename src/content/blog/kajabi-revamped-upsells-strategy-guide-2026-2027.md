## Technical Mechanics: Secure 1-Click Billing Tokens

A frequent point of confusion among creators is how transactions clear without re-prompting the customer for their CVV or billing zip code.

When the customer checks out for the base product, Kajabi Payments or Stripe generates an encrypted payment token (`pm_cust_token`). Under modern card network protocols:
1. The merchant initiates a secondary transaction against the pre-authorized customer token within a designated checkout session window.
2. The transaction complies with 3D Secure 2 (3DS2) and Strong Customer Authentication (SCA) exemptions for merchant-initiated transactions.
3. The customer receives an immediate combined receipt, while Kajabi CRM automations trigger product grants and welcome tags in parallel.

This seamless token handoff removes the primary cause of post-purchase cart abandonment: typing fatigue.

## Practical Deployment: Building a High-Converting Upsell Sequence

To build a professional post-purchase offer flow in your Kajabi admin portal, follow this blueprint:

### Step 1: Craft the Complementary Product
Ensure your upsell logically extends the core purchase. If you just sold a course on social media marketing, the ideal upsell is not an unrelated accounting course; it is a library of 100 customizable Canva graphic templates or an invitation to a live weekly content clinic.

### Step 2: Navigate to Offer Upsell Settings
Open **Sales > Offers** and click on your base product offer. Scroll down to the **Upsell** tab and select **Create Upsell Offer**.

### Step 3: Configure Visual Blocks and Brand Assets
Use the redesigned canvas editor to:
* Embed a short, 90-second video explaining why this add-on accelerates their progress.
* Format three bullet points emphasizing immediate implementation and time savings.
* Set clear pricing contrast showing the special post-purchase discount compared to retail pricing.

### Step 4: Configure the Downsell Logic
If the customer declines your primary upsell, direct them to a downsell offer that provides a lighter alternative (for instance, the audio workbook edition for \$27 or a split 3-pay installment option).

## Ethical Guidelines for High-Trust Upselling

To protect your brand equity while maximizing conversions, adhere to these professional standards:

* **Always Include an Obvious Decline Link:** Never hide the "No thanks, proceed to my course" button. Tricking visitors into buying creates chargebacks and customer resentment.
* **Deliver Instant Value:** Ensure the upsell solves a real problem immediately, rather than feeling like missing puzzle pieces deliberately held back from the main course.
* **Keep Confirmation Clear:** Send an instant itemized invoice so the customer understands their exact billing summary immediately.
