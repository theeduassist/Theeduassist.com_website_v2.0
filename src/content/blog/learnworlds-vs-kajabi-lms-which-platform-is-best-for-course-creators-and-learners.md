Creating an online course platform can be difficult at times. A good course platform should be more than just a place to upload videos. It should help you organize lessons, welcome learners, accept payments, build trust, and support the growth of your course business. Also it should help learners to easily access lessons, easily understand the course structure and complete activities.  
  
Choosing the right learning management system can make major difference when you are planning to build your own course or take an online course   
  
LearnWorlds and Kajabi are two popular options, but both platforms have different  strengths for course creators and learners. For course creators the choice depends on tools for building, marketing, selling, and managing the courses. For learners the experience depends on how courses are organized, how engaging the content is and which tools are available for assessments and progress tracking.

  


**What is LearnWorlds?**  
  
It**** is an online learning platform designed for creators and organizations that want interactive and  structured learning experience. It provides creators with advanced  tools to deliver different types of content, assessing learners and monitoring their progress.  
From a learners point of view, these features can  create a more structured and interactive learning experience because learners can access different types of learning materials, complete assessments, and track their progress through a course.  
  
One of the most notable features is interactive video. It allows course creators to add elements such as questions, buttons, overlays, transcripts, chapters, and learner engagement analytics. This can turn a passive video into a more engaging and interactive learning activity.  
LearnWorlds also supports learning paths, certificates, live sessions, interactive eBooks and SCORM/HTML5 packages. SCORM is a common format for e-learning content. .  
This means the same feature can be beneficial for both sides: creators get more ways to design their lessons, while learners get a more interactive way to learn.  
LearnWorlds may suit:  
Professional training businesses.  
Training or teaching academies.  
Certification programs.  
Corporate learning and developing teams.  
Anyone building an online branded school.  
  


**What is Kajabi?**  
**** On the other hand, Kajabi is an all-in-one platform designed primarily for experts, creators, coaches, educators and entrepreneurs who want to turn their knowledge into an online business. It brings course creation together with website building, landing pages, marketing, email campaigns, sales funnels, payments, coaching, newsletters, and community.  
  
Kajabi is therefore designed for people who are not just interested in teaching but also want to market and sell their courses online. Its main advantage is convenience, especially for new creators. A creator can build a website, collect email addresses, promote an offer, sell a course, and communicate with customers without connecting many separate services. This can make business management simpler, especially for a solo creator or a small team.  
  
Kajabi suits:  
  
Coaches and consultants  
Creators selling courses or other digital products  
Experts who depend on email marketing and funnels  
Entrepreneurs who want several business tools in one platform  
  


So which one is the right option for you? The answer is: choose LearnWorlds if you want to  build a richer and engaging learning environment; and prioritize Kajabi if you want an all-in-one system for marketing and selling your knowledge.   
  
The best choice depends on the kind  of business you want to build, the people you want to teach, and the way you plan to grow. 

**Course Creation and Learning Tools.**

Both LearnWorlds and Kajabi make course creation relatively straightforward. Creators  can upload videos, audios, documents and other learning materials. They also help arrange content into lessons and modules. The difference is the depth of their learning tools.   
From the learner's perspective, however, the quality of the course experience depends on how those materials are presented ,what activities learners can complete and which tools they can use.  
  If your main goal is teaching quality and deep learning activities, LearnWorlds has an  advantage over Kajabi. Its interactive video editor functionality allows creators to place  questions ,buttons, transcripts and other actions directly to video [lessons.it](http://lessons.it) also provides  assessments ,learning paths and tools for organizing a more guided learning.   
For learners, these features can make lessons more engaging and provide more opportunities to interact with the course instead of only watching videos.

 for example these features can be useful for : 

•Skilled based courses 

•Compliance training  

• Teacher led programs  

• Business training  

Kajabi on the other hand makes it easy to organize courses while connecting them with  other products and marketing activities.  
  
Its tools provide quizzes and learning features and are suitable for many creator-led subjects such as fitness, photography, personal development or business education.   
  
Kajabi places less emphasis on specialized e- learning and complex learning activities. Its main purpose is to connect the course with the creator's website, audience, offers and sales process. 

The winner of the learning focused features is LearnWorlds  which can provide more options for trainers or learners who need more advanced learning  activities.   
  
**Learner Experience**  
  
**** Learners may never choose the platform themselves, but the platform affects how they experience a course. A clear layout, useful feedback, progress tracking, mobile access, and opportunities to interact can all help a learner stay involved.

LearnWorlds can provide a more active learning experience. A learner may answer a question during a video, follow a set learning path, complete an assessment, earn a certificate, or join a discussion. These tools can make the course feel more like a guided programme than a video library.

Kajabi offers a clean way to access course lessons, coaching, and community content. It also provides a mobile app, so learners can view content and connect with a community away from a desktop computer. 

  


The quality of the teacher and course content still matters more than the software. A well-planned Kajabi course can be more useful than a poorly planned LearnWorlds course.  
  
 From a platform point of view, however, LearnWorlds offers more tools for active learning, while Kajabi offers a simpler experience connected to the creator’s wider brand and community.

Winner for the learner experience is LearnWorlds, especially for courses that use activities, assessments, and guided progress. Kajabi remains a suitable option for straightforward video courses and creator-led communities.

  


**Email, Marketing and sales** 

Creating a good course is not enough. Creators need tools to build an audience, communicate with potential customers ,collect  email addresses, and make it easy for them to purchase. This is where Kajabi becomes  particularly attractive. 

Kajabi combines course creations with marketing tools such as   
email marketing  
landing  pages  
automated workflows  
checkout pages  
sales funnels ( a funnel is simply a step by  step journey that takes a visitor from interest to action)   
  
 Its current plan  also includes features for communities, websites, marketing emails and sales . 

For example, a visitor can download a free guide, receive a helpful email sequence, attend a webinar and then decide to buy your course. Kajabi can manage all these activities in one platform..  

LearnWorlds also offers tools for selling and promoting courses. Creators can Use  coupons  
 Bundles  
 Subscriptions  
 Affiliate management  
 Marketing related integrations.  
  
 It also  connects with tools such as Zapier, HubSpot, Zoom, WebEx and Mailchimp.  
For learners, these tools are less about the learning experience itself and more about how they discover, purchase, and receive information about a course. 

So the winner for marketing and business growth is Kajabi. It is a stronger choice for creators who want their course, website, email list, and sales process in one system.

**Ease of use**  

Both platforms are designed to help users create courses without requiring advanced  technical knowledge and skill. However the better option depends on what you want to accomplish.    
  
LearnWorlds can be appealing if creator’s main goal is to build an educational experience with engaging content and assessments    
  
Kajabi can be a better choice if you want a central platform for your website, course, marketing emails and other business activities.  
  
 From the learner's perspective, ease of use mainly depends on how the creator organizes or designs the course. A well-structured course with clear lessons and easy navigation can make either platform easier to use.

Because a learner doesn't necessarily experience the entire LMS the way the creator does.

**Analytics and Reporting**  
Analytics and reporting are important for creators because they help them understand how learners are using their courses and whether the content is performing well. However the two platforms focus on different types of information.  
Learnwords provides reports related to learners, courses, assessments and sales. Higher plans include features such as scheduled course reports; these reports can be useful for teachers and training organizations where learners are progressing and where they need support.  
  
Kajabi provides analytics and reporting, but its reporting is more connected to the business side of the platform. Creators can use analytics to monitor sales, customers, offers and course performance.  
  
Choose LearnWorlds when detailed information about the learning experience and learner progress is the key measure of success. For creators who want to connect course performance with sales and business results, Kajabi can be a strong option.  
  
Winner for learning analytics is LearnWorlds.  
Winner for business and sales reporting is Kajabi.  
  
**Integration and Community**  
  


Integrations are useful when creators want their LMS to work with other tools instead of managing everything separately.  
  
 LearnWorlds provides  integrations with different tools and services used for video meetings, marketing, communication, analytics, payment, and automation platforms. It connects with tools such as Zapier, Mailchimp, HubSpot, Zoom, and other services.

Kajabi also provides integrations with a smaller selection of business tools directly and can use Zapier to connect additional services. Since Kajabi already combines websites, email marketing, sales, courses, payments, and community features in one platform, creators may need fewer separate tools for their business.

Both platforms include community features that allow learners to interact with instructors and other learners. LearnWorlds can be useful when community interaction is part of a structured learning experience, while Kajabi communities use groups and spaces and connect them with courses, coaching, memberships, and the creator's wider brand.

From a learner's perspective, community features can make online learning feel less isolated by giving learners opportunities to participate in discussions and connect with others. For creators, integrations and community tools can help connect the course with other business activities and build stronger relationships with learners.

  


Winner: It depends on the creator's needs. LearnWorlds may be better when the community supports a structured course, while Kajabi can be a strong choice for creator-led communities and memberships.

  
  


**Pricing**  

Pricing is an important factor especially for the beginners and one of the biggest differences  between both LMS. pricing can change so the creator must always check the official websites before subscribing.  

LearnWorlds currently lists the following monthly prices:

  


**Starter**: $24 per month: includes one administrator, up to 1,000 active learners per month, and a $5 fee for every enrolment.

**Pro Trainer**: $25 per month: removes enrolment fees and adds features such as subscriptions, certificates, SCORM uploads, more administrators, and more integrations.

**Learning Center**: $249 per month: adds full white-labelling, automation, scheduled course reports, more administrators, and setup support.  
  
Kajabi currently lists these prices for monthly billing:

**Basic**: $143 per month

**Growth**: $199 per month

**Pro**: $399 per month

The plans provide different limits for products, contacts, websites, communities, and  administrators. Kajabi says it does not charge a platform revenue-sharing fee, although normal payment-processing fees may still apply. Annual billing usually lowers the effective monthly cost.

For a beginner with a small budget ,LearnWorlds may be easier to start with because its entry price is much lower but the $5 enrollment fee is worth thinking about.

Kajabi costs more at the start but it may save money for a new creator who would  otherwise pay separately for an email platform, landing page builder, funnel tool and  community tool .  
 To compare value, list every tool you would otherwise need and calculate the total monthly cost. Then choose the platform that provides the features you will use.  
For learners, the platform's subscription price is usually less important because they usually access a course through the creator's chosen platform. However, the creator's platform costs may influence the price of the course or the features available to learners

**Which LMS is better for learners?**  
 If you are a student or a learner, the quality of course content and teaching approach matters greatly along with the platform itself. 

However, from a platform perspective LearnWorlds can provide a more learning focused  environment, with features including interactive video, assessments ,progress tracking, and other engagement tools.   
These features can be particularly useful for learners who prefer courses that involve more than watching recorded videos. Interactive questions, assessments and structured learning activities which can give learners more opportunities to engage in the learning process.  
Kajabi can also provide a smooth learning experience, with organized lessons, videos, quizzes and other course materials. It can work well for learners who prefer a straightforward course structure.

  


Therefore, if the main priority is interactive and structured learning, LearnWorlds is the platform for learners. However, the quality of the actual course still depends heavily on the creator's teaching style and the content they provide.

  


**Which LMS is better for Creators?** 

For creators, the choice depends on the business model. 

++Choose LearnWorlds if:++ 

•You want interactive video features and structured learning paths.  •Your priority is creating interactive educational experiences.   
•You need more advanced assessments and learner analytics.    
•Plan to run a training course. School, academy, or organization with a strong learning focus   
 •Need SCORM or HTML 5 support for professional or corporate learning content. 

++Choose Kajabi if:++ 

•You want to build a knowledge-based business. 

•Marketing and sales are your major priorities. 

•You need integrated email marketing and automation. 

•You want to sell courses alongside other digital products. 

•You prefer having many business tools like landing pages, emails, funnels and community  on one platform. 

**Final Verdict**  
  
There is no single winner for every creator or learner  
  
LearnWorlds is a better choice for learning purposes. Choose it when your main concern is a structured learning environment.  
  
 Kajabi is a better choice for creators focused on marketing and business. Choose it when you want to build a course business using one connected system.  
  
Before deciding answer these questions  
  
1.   [Do](http://1.Do) you need advanced learning activities or mainly video lessons?  
[2.  Do](http://2.Do) you already have marketing tools, or do you want them built into the platform?  
3.  What will the complete setup cost after fees, integrations, and add-ons?  
  
Finally, use a free trial or product demo to build one sample lesson and test the student journey.
