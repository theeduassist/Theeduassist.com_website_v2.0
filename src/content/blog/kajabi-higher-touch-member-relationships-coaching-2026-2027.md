## Architectural Pillars of High-Touch Delivery in Kajabi

Structuring relational programs inside Kajabi requires coordinating several integrated platform features:

### 1. Unified Coaching Portals
Instead of scattering client bookings across Calendly, Zoom, and Google Drive, Kajabi Coaching consolidates the entire engagement:
* **Session Agendas:** Collaborative notes where coach and client outline discussion objectives before each call.
* **Shared Resource Repositories:** Uploading client-specific templates, contracts, and feedback audits into a private document locker.
* **Homework and Milestone Commitments:** Assigning actionable tasks due prior to the subsequent meeting.

### 2. Asynchronous Check-In Cadences
High-touch does not mean living on live video calls. Many busy executives prefer asynchronous check-ins. By embedding short video reviews or voice memos into student lesson submissions, coaches provide meaningful critique on the client timeline.

### 3. CRM Milestone Logging
Coaches can document private notes on the student contact record that remain hidden from the student. Logging personal details (such as current company initiatives or personal goals) ensures that every interaction feels deeply attuned to the client reality.

## 4 Rules for Sustainable High-Touch Coaching

To scale personal relationships without sacrificing personal wellbeing:

1. **Establish Firm Communication Boundaries:** State your response windows clearly (e.g., asynchronous audio replies delivered Tuesdays and Thursdays between 10:00 AM and 2:00 PM).
2. **Leverage Structured Intake Frameworks:** Require clients to submit specific questions and performance figures 24 hours prior to calls, keeping sessions focused.
3. **Celebrate Client Milestones Publicly:** With client permission, share member wins inside your broader community to validate their progress and inspire peers.
4. **Automate the Administrative Routine:** Keep scheduling links, payment invoices, and agreement forms completely automated so all human energy goes into coaching.
