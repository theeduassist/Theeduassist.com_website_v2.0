Creating and selling online courses on WordPress can feel overwhelming. Many course creators, coaches, instructional designers, and L&D teams struggle with clunky tools, high platform fees that eat into revenue, limited customization, and poor student engagement that leads to dropouts. **LifterLMS** offers a practical solution. It is a powerful, flexible WordPress LMS plugin that lets you build, sell, and scale online courses and memberships on your own site. With a free core and smart paid bundles, it gives you ownership without SaaS lock-in or revenue shares.

Whether you are a solo creator or run training for hundreds, it handles the heavy lifting while staying easy to use. Managing memberships, payments, certificates, and community features across multiple plugins often creates technical headaches and inconsistent experiences.

This matters because your time and money are on the line. Learners expect smooth, professional, mobile-friendly experiences that deliver real results. Poor tools lead to refunds, low completion rates, and stalled business growth.

### **What Is LifterLMS?**

**LifterLMS** transforms any WordPress website into a full-featured learning platform. Launched years ago, it remains a top choice in 2026 for its balance of power, flexibility, and affordability.

The core plugin is completely free. You get unlimited courses, lessons, students, memberships, and basic quizzes. Paid bundles add advanced tools for payments, engagement, automation, and more.

It suits many users:

* Course creators and coaches monetizing expertise
* L&D managers and companies for onboarding and compliance training
* Educators and training institutes moving programs online
* WordPress agencies building client sites

### **Key Features of LifterLMS**

**LifterLMS** shines through its comprehensive yet user-friendly feature set.

**Intuitive Course Builder**

Build courses on one screen with drag-and-drop. Organize sections, lessons, quizzes, and assignments easily. Reuse content across courses to save time.

**Multimedia Lessons**: Support video, audio, text, images, and more to suit different learning styles.

**Quizzes and Assessments**

* Basic quizzes in the free core (timed, open-ended)
* Advanced quizzes (paid) add more question types, dynamic content, and detailed grading
**Assignments**: Students upload files for instructor review perfect for practical training.

**Memberships and Access Control**

Built-in memberships let you create tiers with recurring or one-time payments. Restrict content easily with Access Plans. Bundle courses, drip content, and set prerequisites.

**Student Dashboard**

Learners get a clean, central hub to track progress, view courses, grades, memberships, and achievements. It feels professional and keeps students organized.

* My Courses (with progress bars)
* Grades
* Memberships
* Profile and certificates
**Gamification and Certificates**

Award badges, points, and printable certificates automatically. This boosts motivation and provides proof of completion valuable for corporate or compliance training.

**Ecommerce and Payments**

Integrate Stripe, PayPal, WooCommerce, and more. Offer one-time purchases, subscriptions, payment plans, trials, and coupons. Cart abandonment recovery helps recover lost sales.

**Community and Social Learning**

Build discussions, private groups, and social features to foster interaction and reduce isolation in online learning.

**Other Standout Tools**

* Drip content and prerequisites
* Events for live sessions
* Focus Mode for distraction-free learning
* Reporting and analytics
* Integrations with email tools, forms, and page builders (Elementor, etc.)
* Sky Pilot theme for beautiful out-of-the-box design
**LifterLMS** stays secure and updated with regular improvements.


### **LifterLMS Pricing in 2026: Flexible and Transparent**

It uses a freemium model that appeals to beginners and scaling businesses.

* **Core Plugin**: Free forever,  unlimited basics
* **Earth Bundle** (~$199 first year): 1 site, ecommerce essentials (Stripe, PayPal, WooCommerce, cart recovery)
* **Universe Bundle** (~$299 first year, most popular): 5 sites, adds theme, email/CRM, forms
* **Infinity Bundle** (~$799 first year): Unlimited sites, all 15+ advanced add-ons
First-year discounts are often 50% off. Renewals are higher but still competitive. Lifetime options exist for heavy users. 30-day money-back guarantee removes risk.

This structure keeps costs predictable, unlike per-learner SaaS fees. Start free and upgrade as you grow.


### **Step-by-Step: How to Get Started with LifterLMS**

* **Install the Free Core: **Go to WordPress.org, search “LifterLMS”, install and activate. Or download from the official site.
* **Set Up Your Site: **Choose a compatible theme (Sky Pilot recommended) or use your favorite with Elementor or Gutenberg. Create key pages: courses, dashboard, pricing.
* **Build Your First Course**
* Add a new Course

* **Configure Payments: **Add gateways via the appropriate bundle. Test a purchase.

* **Launch and Market: **Use built-in tools or integrations for email and SEO. Track analytics.
**Real Example**: A leadership coach starts with the free core for content, upgrades to Earth Bundle for Stripe payments, and adds Universe features for email automation. They launch in a few weeks and see steady enrollments.

## **FAQs:**


## **1. What is the best free LMS plugin for WordPress?**

The best free WordPress LMS plugins include **LearnPress, Tutor LMS, and LifterLMS (core version)**. LearnPress is beginner-friendly, while Tutor LMS offers a modern interface. However, most advanced features require paid add-ons.


## **2. Which LMS plugin is better: LearnDash or LifterLMS?**

**LearnDash** is best for enterprise-level course structures, advanced quizzes, and scalability.**LifterLMS** is better for coaching, memberships, and all-in-one learning setups. Choice depends on whether you need scalability (LearnDash) or flexibility (LifterLMS).


## **3. Why are WordPress LMS plugins mostly paid?**

WordPress LMS plugins are paid because they require continuous development, updates, hosting compatibility, payment systems, and customer support. Advanced features like certificates, drip content, and analytics also increase costs.


## **4. What are the best WordPress LMS plugins (pros and cons)?**

Top LMS plugins include **LearnDash, LifterLMS, Tutor LMS, LearnPress, and Masteriyo**.

* **LearnDash** → Powerful, but expensive
* **LifterLMS** → Flexible, but add-on heavy
* **Tutor LMS** → Modern UI, but limited free features
* **LearnPress** → Budget-friendly, but less scalable
* **Masteriyo** → Lightweight, but newer ecosystem

## **5. Which plugin can you use to create an LMS in WordPress?**

You can build a full LMS using plugins like **LearnDash, LifterLMS, Tutor LMS, LearnPress, or Masteriyo**. These tools let you create courses, lessons, quizzes, and student dashboards without coding.


## **6. What is the best paid LMS WordPress plugin?**

The best paid LMS plugin is widely considered **LearnDash** because of its advanced course builder, powerful quiz system, and enterprise-level scalability. It is widely used by universities and training platforms.


## **7. In your opinion, which WordPress LMS plugins are best?**

Best LMS plugins by use case:

* LearnDash → Enterprise training
* LifterLMS → Coaching & memberships
* Tutor LMS → Easy setup + modern UI
* LearnPress → Budget-friendly option
* Masteriyo → Lightweight LMS solution

### **Advantages:**

### **Disadvantages:**

* Can become heavy on performance
