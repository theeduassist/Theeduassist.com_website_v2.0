## **What Is a Kajabi Webinar Funnel?**

A **Kajabi webinar funnel** is a structured marketing sequence designed to:

1. Attract visitors
2. Capture registrations
3. Deliver webinar reminders
4. Host or connect the webinar
5. Present an offer
6. Convert attendees into customers
7. Continue nurturing non-buyers

### **Typical Kajabi Webinar Funnel Stages**

Funnel StagePurposeRegistration PageCollect leadsConfirmation PageConfirm attendanceEmail SequenceSend remindersWebinar EventDeliver training/sales presentationOffer PagePresent product/serviceCheckout PageConvert buyersFollow-Up EmailsRecover undecided leads

**Note:** Kajabi funnels are often called **“Pipelines”** inside Kajabi.

## **Step-by-Step Guide**

### **Step 1: Define the Webinar Funnel Goal**

**Common Webinar Funnel Goals:**

- **Lead Generation** — Building email lists and audience growth
- **Course Sales** — Selling online courses and mini-courses
- **High-Ticket Consulting** — Strategy calls and coaching programs
- **Membership Promotion** — Recurring memberships and communities

### **Step 2: Choose the Right Kajabi Funnel Template**

**Recommended Templates:**

- **Webinar OVO Pipeline** (Opt-In → Value → Offer) — Most popular
- **Product Launch Funnel** — Best for multi-day webinars
- **Freebie Funnel** — When paired with lead magnets
- **Coaching Funnel** — For consultants and service providers

### **Step 3: Build High-Converting Funnel Pages**

#### **1. Registration Page**

- Clear webinar title
- Transformation promise
- Speaker credibility
- Date & time details
- Registration form + strong CTA
- Testimonials & key takeaways

#### **2. Thank You / Confirmation Page**

- Registration confirmation
- Calendar save prompts
- Bonus downloads

#### **3. Sales Page**

- Offer breakdown
- Transformation outcomes
- Testimonials
- FAQs
- Bonuses & pricing

#### **4. Checkout Page**

- Trust indicators
- Guarantee statements
- Security badges

### **Step 4: Set Up Email Automation**

**Essential Emails:**

- Registration Confirmation
- Reminder Emails (24h, 1h, 15min before)
- Replay Email
- Offer Emails (benefits + urgency)
- Cart Close Email

### **Step 5: Connect Webinar Hosting Tools**

- Kajabi Events (native)
- Zoom integration
- YouTube Live
- Third-party platforms

### **Step 6: Optimize the Offer Structure**

- Core Product
- Bonus Stack
- Urgency Mechanism (limited-time pricing, deadlines, fast-action bonuses)

### **Step 7: Create Evergreen Webinar Funnels**

Automate registrations, replays, and sequences for 24/7 performance.

### **Step 8: Improve Conversion Rates**

- Strong outcome-focused headlines
- Reduce form fields
- Add social proof
- Segment email lists
- A/B test everything

## **Common Mistakes to Avoid**

- Weak webinar topics
- Poor funnel design
- No email follow-up
- Over-teaching in the webinar
- Weak offers
- No analytics tracking

## **Final Thoughts**

Building a successful Kajabi webinar funnel in 2026 requires strategy, automation, and continuous optimization. Kajabi simplifies the process, but strong messaging remains essential.
