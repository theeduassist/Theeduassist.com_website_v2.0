## **What Is Teachable and Why Consider It?**

Teachable is a popular online course platform designed for creators, coaches, educators, and businesses to build, market, and sell digital courses, coaching programs, and downloads. It provides hosting, payment processing, student management, and marketing tools in one place.

It stands out for its user-friendly interface, making it accessible even for those without technical skills. Whether you’re a solopreneur sharing knowledge or an organization scaling training, it handles the backend so you can concentrate on content quality.


## **Key Features of Teachable**

Teachable offers a robust set of tools tailored for course success:

* **Drag-and-drop course builder**  Easily organize lessons, videos, quizzes, and text without coding.
* **AI-powered tools**  Generate course outlines, quizzes, subtitles, and translations to save time.
* **Student experience**  Clean, mobile-friendly interface with progress tracking and certificates.
* **Sales and marketing** Built-in checkout, coupons, upsells, affiliate programs, and email tools.
* **Payments and compliance**  Handles taxes (including VAT), multiple payment gateways, and secure transactions.
* **Analytics**  Track student engagement, sales, and completion rates.
These features make it practical for different user types, from independent coaches to training institutes.


## **Teachable Pricing Plans in 2026**

Understanding costs helps you choose the right fit. Teachable offers tiered plans (pricing is approximate and subject to change - check the official site for latest details):

* **Starter**  Entry-level option with basic features and transaction fees.
* **Builder**  Removes most fees, adds more products, and includes core selling tools.
* **Growth** Advanced marketing, affiliates, and branding options for scaling creators.
* **Advanced/Business**  Higher limits, team features, and priority support for larger operations.
Many users appreciate the shift toward no-transaction-fee plans at higher tiers, though some note recent adjustments made lower plans less attractive for beginners. Annual billing often provides discounts.

**Pros of Teachable pricing:**

* Unlimited students on paid plans
* Clear path to remove platform branding
* Integrated payment handling reduces admin work
**Cons:**

* No truly free plan for serious selling anymore
* Transaction fees on entry plans add up
* Price jumps between tiers can feel significant

## **Step-by-Step: How to Create a Course on Teachable**

Getting started is straightforward:

* Sign up and set up your school  Choose a subdomain or connect a custom domain.
* Create your course  Add title, description, and thumbnail.
* Build content Upload videos, add text, PDFs, quizzes, and assignments using the drag-and-drop editor.
* Design sales pages  Customize with AI assistance for compelling copy and layouts.

## **Whatever Happened to the Term “Teachable Moment”?**

The term **“teachable moment”** is still widely used in education, parenting, coaching, and training. It refers to an unplanned opportunity when a learner is highly ready and motivated to understand a new concept or lesson.


### **Origin and Current Use**

* Popularized in the 1950s by educator Robert Havighurst.
* It highlights those special times when real-life events create perfect conditions for meaningful learning.
* You’ll still see it in teaching strategies, classroom guides, and modern parenting advice.

### **Why It Might Seem Less Common**

Structured online courses and standardized programs can reduce spontaneous opportunities. Yet the concept remains powerful. Good platforms help you design flexible content that captures these moments through engaging videos, real examples, and interactive elements.
