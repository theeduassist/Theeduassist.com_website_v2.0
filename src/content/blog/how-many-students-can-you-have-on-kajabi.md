If you are planning to launch an ++[online course](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/theeduassist_realestate-instructionaldesign-elearning-activity-7501866712150073344-yPd8?utm_source=chatgpt.com)++, one of the first questions you may have is: how many students can you have on Kajabi? The answer is more complicated than a simple maximum number of students because Kajabi currently structures its plans around contacts, rather than giving each plan a universal student-per-course limit.

According to Kajabi's current pricing information, the Basic plan includes 2,500 contacts, Growth includes 25,000 contacts, and Pro includes 100,000 contacts. The plans also differ in the number of products, websites, communities, and admin users they support.

So, if you are asking how many students Kajabi can handle, the practical answer is that it can support a very large student base, depending on your total contact count and the plan you choose.

## **Does Kajabi Have a Student Limit?**

Kajabi does not currently advertise one simple Kajabi student limit that applies to every course. Instead, the important capacity number is your account's contact limit.

This is important, as a student is just one kind of contact. There can be people in your Kajabi account that subscribed to your email list, downloaded a free resource from your site, purchased a different product, joined your community, or joined a course. 

For example, imagine you have:

- 1,000 course students
- 3,000 email subscribers
- 2,000 prospects
- 500 customers who purchased another product

Your business could have thousands more contacts than actual students. That is why you should not assume that a 2,500-contact Kajabi plan means you can have exactly 2,500 students. Your actual student capacity depends on how your audience is distributed across your account.

So, **how many students can Kajabi handle?** Kajabi's current plans provide a substantial amount of room for ++[online education businesses](https://medium.com/@hafsazahoor144/10-best-learning-management-systems-compared-c4498d4c02f5?sharedUserId=hafsazahoor144)++.

These are the current limits displayed by Kajabi. The Basic plan is designed for a smaller business and supports up to 2,500 contacts. Growth increases that capacity to 25,000 contacts, while Pro increases it to 100,000.

This means Kajabi can potentially support thousands or even tens of thousands of students, provided your total contact database remains within your plan's limit.

## **Understanding the Kajabi Contact Limit**

The Kajabi contact limit is one of the most important numbers to understand before choosing a plan. Think of contacts as the people in your business database. Not everyone in that database has to be an enrolled student.

A person may find your webpage via Google and enroll in your absolutely free guide. Another person can sign up for your newsletter. Someone else may buy your course right this moment. 

All of these people can become part of your audience, even though only some eventually become students. This is why a course creator with 1,000 students might need more capacity than another creator with 1,500 students. If the first creator has 20,000 marketing contacts while the second has only 1,700 total contacts, their platform requirements are completely different.

## **How Many Students Does Kajabi Allow on Basic?**

The current Kajabi Basic plan supports **2,500 contacts** and up to **5 products**. Kajabi currently lists Basic at $179 per month when billed monthly, or $143 per month when billed annually.

For a new course creator, this can be plenty of capacity. Suppose you have one flagship course and expect to build an audience gradually. You might have 300 students, 500 prospects, and another 400 email subscribers. Your total audience would still be comfortably below the 2,500-contact limit.

But you need to consider the future growth prospects, not just the current numbers. Building a database of contacts is easy if you have a ++[successful campaign](https://medium.com/@rimshasshahid/how-to-build-a-massive-high-ticket-funnel-in-20-days-4230dc7923b6?sharedUserId=hafsazahoor144)++ or free lead magnet. 

Basic is therefore best suited to creators who have a relatively focused product catalog and a contact database that comfortably fits within the 2,500 limit.

## **How Many Students Does Kajabi Allow on Growth?**

The Growth plan increases the contact limit significantly to **25,000 contacts** and supports up to **50 products**. Kajabi currently lists Growth at $249 per month when billed monthly, or $199 per month when billed annually. This makes Growth much more suitable for an established ++[course business](https://www.theeduassist.com/blog/how-to-sell-online-courses-that-actually-convert/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)++.

For example, imagine that you have 5,000 students across several courses but another 10,000 people in your marketing database. You could still have substantial room before reaching the 25,000-contact limit.

Growth is also beneficial when your business is larger than just one course. Your courses can be beginner or advanced, membership, workshops, coaching, and more- all digital products.  With a 50-product allowance, Growth provides significantly more room for this type of expansion than Basic.

## **What Is the Kajabi Student Limit on Pro?**

The Pro plan provides the largest standard contact capacity, with **100,000 contacts** and unlimited products. Kajabi currently lists Pro at $499 per month when billed monthly, or $399 per month when billed annually.

This is designed for businesses operating at a much larger scale. For those with tens of thousands of contacts, more than one course, more than one community, more than one website, or more than one team, there is significantly more capacity available with Pro. 

Again, however, 100,000 contacts does not necessarily mean 100,000 students in one course. You could distribute 100,000 contacts across prospects, customers, subscribers, students, and other members of your audience.

The key takeaway is that Pro provides a large education enterprise a great deal of flexibility to expand without hitting the typical contact limit that the standard platform has. 

## **Kajabi Customer Limit vs. Student Limit**

You may also encounter the term Kajabi customer limit when researching this topic. This can create confusion because older Kajabi pricing information and articles may refer to different limits than the current plans.

Kajabi changed its pricing structure and introduced updated plans in January 2026, as part of ++[Kajabi's 2026 pricing changes](https://www.kajabi.com/updates/2025-pricing-updates?utm_source=chatgpt.com)++. The current pricing page prominently lists contact limits of 2,500, 25,000, and 100,000 across Basic, Growth, and Pro.

Therefore, if you find an older article claiming that a particular Kajabi plan has a different customer or active-customer limit, check the date before relying on it. For current planning, the official Kajabi pricing page is the better reference.

## **Can Kajabi Handle Thousands of Students?**

Yes. Kajabi's current contact limits make it capable of supporting businesses with thousands of learners. The Growth plan provides capacity for up to 25,000 contacts, while Pro provides up to 100,000. But technical capacity is only one part of scaling an online course.

With thousands of students in your hands, you need to have effective onboarding, automated communication, organized course content, customer support, ++[payment processing](https://www.theeduassist.com/blog/what-payment-methods-does-kajabi-accept/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)++, and a clear student experience. You can have a platform that can hold a ton of people, but to sustain your business operations, you need to grow with the platform. 

This is where Kajabi's broader platform approach can be useful. ++[Kajabi for educators](https://www.kajabi.com/for/educators?utm_source=chatgpt.com)++ can be especially relevant for creators who want to manage their educational content, students, and online business from one platform. ## **What Happens When You Reach Your Kajabi Contact Limit?**

When users are approaching the contact limit, you need to review your account before you can start another large marketing campaign. The first step is to review your current database and see if your current contacts are still relevant to your business. 

Contacts should not be deleted randomly, however, particularly if they're linked to customers, purchases, or essential files. If you genuinely need more capacity, upgrading to a higher plan is the straightforward option. Kajabi's current standard progression is:

**2,500 → 25,000 → 100,000 contacts.**

Kajabi has also announced options for additional contact capacity and stated that the price for an additional 5,000 contacts increased from $20 to $25 as part of its 2026 pricing changes. Because pricing and available options can change, check your current account and Kajabi's pricing information before making a purchase decision.

## **Which Kajabi Plan Is Best for Your Students?**

The easiest way to choose is to look at your total contacts, not just your current student count. If you are launching your first course and have a relatively small audience, Basic may be enough. Growth offers much more space when considering a larger course business that has thousands of contacts and multiple products.

If you have a large education business and hundreds of thousands of contacts, and you have a lengthy product catalog, then Pro might be the better option. The point is to leave the plan some room to grow. You don't want to pick a plan that is already full on the day you start a successful campaign. 

## **Conclusion**

So, how many students can you have on Kajabi? There is no single universal student number because Kajabi's current plans are primarily based on contact limits. Basic supports 2,500 contacts, Growth supports 25,000, and Pro supports 100,000. The plans also provide different product limits, with 5 products on Basic, 50 on Growth, and unlimited products on Pro.

Your actual number of students can therefore be considerably different from your contact limit. If you have a small course and a growing audience, Basic may be sufficient. When your business is gaining thousands of learners and leads, Growth provides you with a lot more space. Pro offers the maximum contact capacity for a large-scale education business, as well as unlimited products. 

The smartest approach is simple: calculate your total contacts, estimate your future growth, and then choose the Kajabi plan that gives your business enough room to scale.
