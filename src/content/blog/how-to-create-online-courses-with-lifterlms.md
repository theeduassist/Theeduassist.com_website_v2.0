## **How to create e-learning Courses with LifterLMS?**

It’s very easy to create a course on LifterLMS, when you know each and every step.


### **1: Install LifterLMS**

The LifterLMS plugin is something you need to get started with. It is the first thing you should do. You will find the option to install the LifterLMS plugin, in your WordPress dashboard.

Once you’ve launched the setup wizard, it will lead you through the initial setup, including course pages and student account creation.


### **2: Build Your Course Structure**

Plan your course before uploading content.

The basic structure would appear to be:

**Course ElementPurpose**SectionsOrganize major topicsLessonsDeliver learning contentQuizzesAssess understandingAssignmentsEncourage practical applicationCertificatesCompletion of reward

A good course plan helps you learn better and finish it often.


### **3: Add Lessons and Learning Materials**

Now that your plan is set, start adding lessons and stuff to learn from.

You can include:

* Video lessons
* Written tutorials
* Downloadable resources
* Worksheets
* Presentations
* Audio files
Successful online courses do this. They have things that work for everyone so people can learn the way they like.


### **4: Generate quizzes and reviews.**

Assessments help learners to measure their progress.

With LifterLMS, you can build:

* Quizzes (multiple choice)
* Timed assessments
* Pass/fail requirements
* Lesson completion criteria
This helps students stay interested and excited about the course.It keeps them engaged and motivated throughout.


### **5: Set Pricing**

Next, decide how learners will access your course.

Popular options include:

* A one-time purchase
* Monthly subscription
* Annual membership
* Bundled course packages
You need to pick a model that’s really important to the people you want to sell to and what your company wants to do.


### **6: Launch and Promote**

Now that everything is tested it’s time to publish your course.You should start promoting it away.

You can attract learners through:

* Blog content
* Email marketing
* Social media platforms
* Digital communities
* Webinars
* Search engine optimization
A successful promotional approach is equally essential as the course itself.

## **What Are the Mistakes to Avoid When Designing Online Courses?**

Many unskilled course developers commit similar mistakes.

Avoiding them can save time and increase learner satisfaction.


### **Publishing Too Much Content**

Much content doesn’t always mean better learning.

You see, when there is much information it can actually confuse learners.

Focus on helping learners achieve a specific goal.Make sure your content is easy to understand and not too long.


### **Ignoring Course Structure**

A confusing course layout can be really frustrating, for people. This often leads to low engagement.

To avoid this you should make navigation simple. Organize the lessons in a logical way. This will make the course layout easier to follow and understand.


### **Skipping student’s Feedback**

Feedback helps find out what’s missing in your course.

You should collect what students think often and use it to make versions better.

Also getting student insights often can really help.


### **Ignoring Mobile Users**

Many students use cellphones to access the courses.

You should always try them out on your smartphone, tablet and computer to see if lectures, videos and quizzes are working properly.
