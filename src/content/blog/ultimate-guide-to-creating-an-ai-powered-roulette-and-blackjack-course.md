## **Step-by-Step Guide to Build AI-Powered Roulette & Blackjack Course**

### **1. Define Learning Goals**

First, clearly define what students will learn.

For instance:

- Roulette strategies
- Blackjack rules
- Probability basics

Clear goals make your AI-Powered Roulette & Blackjack Course more effective.

### **2. Create Course Structure**

Now organize your course into modules.


| Module | Topic | Learning Outcome |
| ------ | ------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 | Basics | Understand basic casino environments and bankroll foundations. |
| 2 | Rules | Learn complete gameplay rules for both roulette and blackjack. |

| 4 | Probability | Understand house edge, risk assessment, and calculating odds. |
| 5 | Practice | Improve real-time betting skills using interactive simulation drills. |
| 6 | Responsible Gaming | Learn safe gaming habits, setting limits, and emotional control. |


This structure makes your AI-Powered Roulette & Blackjack Course easy to follow.
