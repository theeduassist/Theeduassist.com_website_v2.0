## **FAQ’s**


### **What is the first step to optimize my Kajabi landing page for conversions?**

The **first step** is to define a clear goal and understand your audience before touching the editor.

**Why this matters:** Without a single focused objective, your **custom kajabi landing page** will confuse visitors and hurt conversions.

**How to do it:**

* Decide the primary goal (e.g., course enrollment, lead capture, webinar sign-up).
* Identify your ideal learner’s biggest pain point.
* Craft a benefit-driven headline that speaks directly to that pain.
**Pro Tip for Learning Professionals:** Use Action Mapping (great for Instructional Designers) to keep the **kajabi landing page** focused on one outcome. This simple step alone can dramatically improve results on your **landing page kajabi**.


### **What makes a strong CTA on a Kajabi landing page?**

A strong CTA on your **kajabi landing page** is clear, benefit-focused, and action-oriented.

**Characteristics of a High-Converting CTA:**

* Uses strong action words: “Enroll Now”, “Get Instant Access”, “Claim Your Spot”, “Start Learning Today”
* Highlights the value: “Download Free Checklist” instead of just “Download”
* Stands out visually with contrasting colors and large buttons
* Creates mild urgency: “Limited Seats – Reserve Now”
* Placed strategically (above the fold + repeated throughout the page)
**Best Practice:** Keep only one primary CTA per **kajabi landing page** to avoid decision paralysis. Test variations using Kajabi’s A/B testing tools.


### **Should I use video on my landing page, and where?**

**Yes**, video is highly recommended on **kajabi landing pages** when used strategically.

**Where to Use Video:**

* **Above the fold (Hero Section):** A short welcome video (30–90 seconds) explaining the transformation.
* **Problem & Solution Section:** Video showing real learner results or your teaching style.
* **Testimonials:** Video testimonials build massive trust.
* **Before Final CTA:** A personal message from you as the instructor.
**Tips for Success:**

* Keep videos under 2 minutes for best engagement.
* Add a custom thumbnail.
* Use Kajabi’s native video uploader for faster loading.
* Test with and without video — some audiences prefer fast-loading image heroes.
**For Coaches & Educators:** A personal video makes your **custom kajabi landing page** feel more human and increases connection.


### **How do I design a landing page in Kajabi?**

Designing a **kajabi landing page** is straightforward using Kajabi’s drag-and-drop builder.

**Step-by-Step Process:**

* Go to **Website → Landing Pages** in your Kajabi dashboard.
* Click **+ New Landing Page**.
* Choose a template (Sales, Opt-in, Webinar, etc.).
* Enter a page title and click **Create**.
* Customize using the visual editor:
* Add/rearrange sections (hero, testimonials, curriculum, FAQ, etc.)
* Edit text, images, buttons, and colors.
* Switch between desktop and mobile views.
* Set SEO settings and publish.
**Quick Design Tips for Strong Kajabi Landing Page Design:**

* Use plenty of white space.
* Maintain consistent branding.
* Make buttons large and contrasting.
* Optimize fully for mobile.
Kajabi’s templates are already conversion-focused, so start with one and customize it.


### **How do I increase conversions on a Kajabi landing page?**

To boost conversions on your **kajabi landing pages**, follow these proven tactics:

* **Strong Hero Section:** Benefit-focused headline + clear CTA above the fold.
* **Build Trust:** Add testimonials, case studies, and guarantees near every CTA.
* **Scannable Copy:** Short paragraphs, bullets, and subheadings.
* **Social Proof:** Real results and learner stories.
* **Urgency & Scarcity:** Limited spots, bonuses, or countdowns.
* **A/B Testing:** Test headlines, images, and CTAs in Kajabi.
* **Mobile Optimization:** Ensure perfect experience on phones.
* **Fast Loading:** Compress images and limit heavy elements.
**Realistic Expectation:** Small changes like a better headline or added video testimonial can increase conversions by 20–40%.


### **How do I set a landing page as my homepage?**

Setting a **kajabi landing page** as your homepage is simple.

**Step-by-Step:**

* Go to **Settings → Site Details** in your Kajabi dashboard.
* Scroll to the **Homepage** section.
* Click the dropdown and select **Show a Landing Page**.
* Choose the specific **kajabi landing page** you want as your homepage.
* Click **Save**.
This is perfect for sales-focused sites where you want every visitor to land directly on your best-converting **custom kajabi landing page**.
