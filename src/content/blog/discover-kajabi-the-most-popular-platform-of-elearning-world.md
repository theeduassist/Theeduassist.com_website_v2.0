### **What Is Kajabi?**

Kajabi is a complete operating system designed for knowledge businesses. It allows experts, trainers, coaches, and organizations to build, market, sell, and deliver digital products without switching between different apps.

It excels at professional course hosting, beautiful website creation, email marketing, sales pipelines, and member communities.


### **Key Kajabi Features**

**Course Creation & Delivery**

* Easy video hosting and organization
* Quizzes, drip content, progress tracking
* Support for PDFs, audio, and interactive elements
**Websites & Templates**Kajabi offers professional templates that help you create conversion-focused websites quickly. You can customize them to match your brand without needing advanced coding skills on higher plans.

**Marketing & Sales Tools**

* Email marketing capabilities
* Pipeline builder for automated sales funnels
* Integrated payment processing
* Detailed analytics dashboard
**Communities**Built-in community features encourage discussions, live sessions, and peer interaction, which significantly improves learner retention.

**Branded Mobile App**For an additional fee (around $199/month), you can launch your own branded app so students can access content, community, and courses directly on their phones with push notifications.

**Other Features**

* Clean admin dashboard
* API access for custom integrations
* Strong help center and support resources

### **Kajabi Pricing Plans:**

Kajabi offers flexible plans based on your business stage. Prices are lower with annual billing.

* **Starter / Kickstarter**: Best for beginners (limited products & contacts)  starts around $89/month
* **Basic**: More products and contacts  roughly $143–179/month (annual)
* **Growth**: Higher limits and advanced features  around $199–249/month
* **Pro**: Unlimited products, more websites & communities  around $399–499/month
**Basic Plan Limitations**: Restricted number of products, contacts, and team members. Most growing businesses eventually upgrade.

**Branded App Cost**: Approximately $199/month extra.

Kajabi occasionally offers free trials or promo codes. Always verify current pricing on their official site.
