## **How to Create an Account on Teachable**

**Step 1:** Visit [teachable.com](https://teachable.com/) and click “Start free trial” for a 7-day no-risk test.

**Step 2:** Enter your name, email, and password. Confirm via email.

**Step 3:** Set up your school name and customize your subdomain (e.g., [yourschool.teachable.com](http://yourschool.teachable.com)).

**Step 4:** Choose a plan during onboarding **Starter at $39/mo** suits beginners with basic tools.

**Quick setup checklist:**

- Verify email immediately
- Skip advanced settings initially
- Explore the dashboard for quick navigation

**Real example:** Freelance ID started her L&D course school in under 5 minutes, avoiding complex setups like on Kajabi.

## **How to Create a Course on Teachable**

**Step 1:** From your dashboard, go to **“Courses” > “New Course.”** Name it and add a description.

**Step 2:** **Outline using AI:** Enter a prompt like *“Instructional design basics for beginners,”* and it generates sections, lessons, and sample content.

**Three creation options:**

- **Start from scratch:** Add sections manually
- **Bulk upload:** Teachable creates lessons per file
- **Copy from existing courses**

**Pro tip for L&D managers:** Add quizzes for Kirkpatrick Level 1 evaluation right away.

**Example:** Corporate trainer created a *“Remote Team Engagement”* course with 5 AI-generated modules, saving hours on ADDIE analysis.

## **How to Upload Course Content (Videos, PDFs)**

**Step 1:** In the course editor, add lessons under sections. Click **“Add Lesson”**.

**Step 2:** Upload from computer, Google Drive, Dropbox, or URL.

**Supported formats:**

- **Videos:** MP4 up to 2GB recommended; auto-subtitles available on higher plans
- **PDFs:** Direct upload for downloads
- **Images/Audio:** Embed seamlessly

**Best practices:**

- Organize files as *“Section1-Lesson1.mp4”* for bulk imports
- Preview on mobile for accessibility

**Pro tip for educators:** Use drip content to release videos weekly, boosting retention by 35% in one coach’s experience.

**Example:** Upload a PDF workbook on microlearning hacks alongside a 10-min video—students download instantly.

## **How to Publish and Launch Your Course**

**Step 1:** **Preview curriculum** check flow, quizzes, and mobile view under “Curriculum.”

**Step 2:** **Set live** toggle **“Published”** in settings.

**Step 3:** Customize sales page with templates, testimonials, and urgency timers.

**Launch checklist:**

- Email beta testers via built-in tools
- Share link on LinkedIn/Facebook
- Use abandoned cart recovery

**For institute owners:** Pilot with 50 users for compliance testing.

**Success example:** Coach launched *“Leadership Mastery”* to 200 sign-ups in week 1 via free module teaser.

## **Frequently Asked Questions (Teachable Course)**

### **What do I get when I enroll?**

You get instant access to all course materials, including lessons, downloadable resources, and any bonuses listed on the page.

### **Is this course beginner-friendly?**

Yes. The course is designed step-by-step, making it easy to follow even if you’re starting from zero.

### **How long will I have access?**

Access depends on the plan you choose. Check the pricing section for full details before enrolling.

### **Will this work on mobile?**

Yes. The course is fully optimized for mobile, so you can learn anytime, anywhere.

### **Can I preview the course before buying?**

Yes. If previews are enabled, you can explore parts of the curriculum before making a purchase.

### **Do you offer refunds?**

Refund policies are clearly mentioned on the sales or checkout page please review them before enrolling.

### **Can I pay in installments?**

If installment options are available, they will be shown in the pricing section at checkout.

### **How do I access the course after payment?**

Once you complete your purchase, you’ll receive instant access through your account along with a confirmation email.
