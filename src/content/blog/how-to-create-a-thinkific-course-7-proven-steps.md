## **How To Create a Thinkific Course?**

## **Step 1: Nail Your Audience Personas:**

Great courses sell because they solve *specific* pains. Use our 5 personas framework refined from LinkedIn Workplace Learning Reports (LinkedIn, 2025) to target content that converts.

- **Instructional Designers**: Need templates for interactivity on deadlines. Write: “Drag this Thinkific quiz block here for 20% better retention.”
- **L&D Managers**: Crave ROI data. Share: “Kirkpatrick-proven metrics from dashboards.”
- **Educators & Trainers**: Want engagement hacks. Offer: “Branching scenarios that feel personal.”
- **Course Creators**: Seek sales boosters. Give: “Pricing tiers that cut refunds by 50%.”
- **Decision Makers**: Demand scalability. Provide: “How we scaled a 5,000-learner program.”

## **Step 2: Design Massive Thinkific ECourses with Proven Structures**

Massive doesn’t mean endless videos. It means *valuable* structured for completion and upsells. Use SAM (Successive Approximation Model) over rigid ADDIE for speed.

Break into 7-12 min modules: Hook (30s story) + Teach (core skill) + Practice (quiz/activity) + Apply (downloadable). Research from *Journal of Research on Technology in Education* shows this cuts drop-offs 42% (Hedberg, 2022).

**For Educators**: Add branching scenarios. Example: “If learner picks ‘budget trainer,’ route to low-cost hacks.”**Tactical Tip**: Use  lesson types video, PDF, live Zoom for hybrid appeal.

## **Step 3: How much does Thinkific cost? Boost Engagement with Interactivity**

Boring courses flop. Thinkific’s tools make yours sticky quizzes, assignments, discussions for 3x retention (*Educational Technology & Society*, 2024).

### **Zero-Budget Interactivity Stack**

1. **Quizzes**: 70% pass threshold unlocks next lesson. IDs: Auto-grade saves hours.
2. **Drag-and-Drop**: For trainers, simulate real tasks (e.g., “Build your sales script”).
3. **Communities**: Thinkific forums foster connection coaches report 50% higher loyalty.
4. **Downloads**: Checklists, swipe files. Creators: Brand them for viral shares.

**Example for Course Creators**: “Module 3: Price Your Course.” Quiz: “Match pain to price tier.” Boom learners self-sell.

## **Step 4: Sales Funnels That Converts (ROI for Managers)**

Courses sell via psychology, not hype. Build funnels: Free lead magnet → Low-ticket course → High-ticket mastery.

- **Free**: 20-min webinar (60% opt-in rate).
- **$97**: Core course (10 modules).
- **$497**: VIP with coaching calls.
- **$2k+**: Group program.

Data from TalentLMS Benchmark (2025) shows tiered pricing lifts revenue 55%. For decision makers, this scales: One institute sold 500 spots at $197 via Thinkific.

## **How much does Thinkific cost?**

**Thinkific Pricing Plans:**

It offers a flexible, tiered pricing model designed for course creators at every stage, with plans starting at **$49/month (Basic)**, followed by **Start ($99/month)** and **Grow ($199/month)**, plus a custom **Plus plan for enterprises**. All plans include unlimited courses and typically **0% platform transaction fees**, making it scalable for growing businesses. 

However, key features like memberships, certificates, live lessons, and advanced integrations are only unlocked in higher tiers, meaning most serious creators eventually upgrade. Additionally, while the free plan has been removed, it now offers a trial period, and costs may vary depending on payment processors or add-ons.

**Thinkific Basic Plan:**

This is an entry-level option designed for new course creators who want to launch and validate their online courses. It includes **unlimited courses and students**, a **custom domain**, basic **site-building tools**, and essential **sales features** like coupons, order bumps, and gifting.

This plan also provides **email support, analytics, and a simple community feature**, making it suitable for creators building their first structured learning experience.

However, advanced instructional design features like **certificates, assignments, memberships, live lessons, and automation tools are not included** and are available in higher plans.Overall, the Basic plan is best for beginners who want to **start quickly, test course ideas, and build a foundation before scaling**.

**Thinkific Plus Pricing:**

**Thinkific Plus** is an enterprise-level plan with **custom pricing**, typically starting from a high monthly investment based on business size and requirements.

It includes advanced features like **SSO, APIs, bulk enrollments, white-label branding, dedicated success support, and enterprise security**. Best for large organizations and academies needing scalable, high-performance learning infrastructure.
