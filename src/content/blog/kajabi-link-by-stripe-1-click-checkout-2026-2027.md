## Frontline Implementation: Activating Link in Kajabi

Activating Link by Stripe within your Kajabi architecture requires minimal technical configuration. Follow these key steps:

### 1. Verify Payment Processor Integration
Navigate to **Settings > Payment Integrations**. Ensure your account is connected to either **Kajabi Payments** or your dedicated **Stripe** account. Link operates as a native component of modern Stripe billing components.

### 2. Enable Link in Checkout Settings
Under your checkout appearance and payment method toggles, verify that the **Link by Stripe** option is toggled to active. Modern Kajabi themes automatically render the recognizable Link badge adjacent to the email field.

### 3. Streamline Form Fields
To maximize the power of Link, remove unnecessary required fields from your checkout forms. If you sell digital courses or downloadable templates, disable requirements for phone numbers or physical mailing addresses unless legally required for tax calculations. The fewer fields you present, the more explosive the Link conversion lift.

### 4. Test the End-User Experience
Perform a live purchase test using an incognito window with a mobile device. Observe how typing your email immediately prompts the 6-digit Link authentication code, automatically populating the stored credit card with zero manual friction.

## Security, Privacy, and Financial Compliance

A common concern among course creators is whether accelerated checkout networks introduce financial risk or compliance vulnerabilities. Link adheres to the highest global security standards:

* **PCI Service Provider Level 1:** The most stringent certification level available in the global payments industry.
* **Tokenized Data Transmission:** Actual card numbers are never stored on Kajabi servers or exposed to your agency team. Transactions use encrypted cryptographic tokens.
* **Two-Factor Authentication (2FA):** Every transaction conducted on a new browser or device requires an instant SMS code or biometric verification, effectively eliminating stolen card fraud.
* **Full Refund and Dispute Compatibility:** Refunds, chargebacks, and dunning workflows operate identically to standard Stripe credit card payments inside your Kajabi dashboard.
