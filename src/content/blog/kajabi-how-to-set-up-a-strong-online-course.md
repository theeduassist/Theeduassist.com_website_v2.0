A smooth, professional platform builds instant trust, keeps students engaged, and lets you focus on teaching instead of fixing tech issues.

### **Why Choose Kajabi for Your Online Course?**

Kajabi stands out as a complete business operating system designed for knowledge creators. You can build, sell, and deliver online courses, memberships, coaching programs, and communities without switching between multiple tools.

**Key benefits include**:

* Professional course delivery with drip content, quizzes, progress tracking, and certificates
* Built-in marketing tools like landing pages, email automation, and sales funnels
* Seamless payments with support for one-time fees, subscriptions, and payment plans
* Branded mobile app experience for students (on eligible plans)
* Modern templates and AI features to speed up creation
Kajabi removes complexity so you can concentrate on creating impactful learning experiences.


### **Step 1: Plan Your Course Before You Begin**

Success starts with solid planning outside the platform.

* Clearly define your target audience and their biggest challenges
* Map their starting point (Point A) to the desired transformation (Point Z)
* Outline modules and lessons that deliver real results
* Choose content formats: videos, worksheets, audio, quizzes, or downloads
* Validate demand through audience surveys or market research
**Pro tip from TheEduAssist**: Keep your course focused. A tight, high-value program (4–8 modules) often performs better than an overloaded one.

**Real example**: A career coach creating a “Job Search Mastery” course planned modules around mindset, resume building, interview skills, and negotiation. Each module contained 4–6 short, actionable lessons.


### **Step 2: Sign Up and Set Up Your Kajabi Account**

* Go to Kajabi.com and start your free trial.
* Enter your business details and verify your email.
* Connect your custom domain (recommended for branding).
* Set up payment processors  Stripe and PayPal integrate easily.
* Explore the clean dashboard with sections for Products, Marketing, Sales, and Contacts.
Take time to familiarize yourself with the interface. The platform feels intuitive even for non-technical users.


### **Step 3: Create Your First Course Product(kajabi Online Courses):**

* Navigate to **Products → + Create New → Course**.
* Select a template: Online Course, Drip Course, Membership, or start blank.
* Enter a clear, benefit-driven title (e.g., “Build Your First Website in 21 Days”).
* Write a compelling description focused on student outcomes.
* Upload an attractive course thumbnail.
* Set your pricing strategy (one-time, monthly, or payment plans).
Kajabi’s setup wizard makes this process straightforward.


### **Step 4: Build and Organize Your Course Content(Setup online Course Kajabi):**

This is where Kajabi truly shines with its user-friendly editor.

**Inside the course builder**:

* Add **Modules** to group related content (like book chapters)
* Create **Lessons** within each module
* Upload videos, add text, PDFs, audio files, or external embeds
* Include quizzes to reinforce learning and track progress
* Control release settings: immediate, scheduled drip, or locked until previous lessons are completed
**Practical steps**:

* Click into your new course.
* Add and name your first module.
* Add lessons one by one.
* Upload or record videos directly in the platform.
* Attach supporting materials.
* Use the preview mode to experience it as a student.
**Engagement tips**:

* Keep videos between 5–15 minutes
* Add downloadable resources in every module
* Use progress bars and completion certificates
* Enable community discussions for peer support
**TheEduAssist client example**: We helped a leadership trainer lock modules behind quiz completion. This simple change increased course completion rates by more than 40%.


### **Step 5: Customize Design and Student Experience**

Make your course reflect your unique brand:

* Adjust colors, fonts, and layout to match your website
* Add a welcome video or personalized onboarding message
* Design a clean student dashboard
* Activate the Kajabi branded app so students can learn on mobile
No coding is required. Kajabi’s templates give you a polished, professional look instantly.


### **Step 6: Set Up Offers, Payments, and Checkouts**

* Create an **Offer** connected to your course
* Define price, payment options, and any upsells or order bumps
* Build a simple sales page or use the built-in checkout
* Add discount codes for special launches
Test the entire purchase flow in preview mode before going live.


### **Step 7: Market and Automate Your Course**

Kajabi’s marketing tools help you attract and nurture students:

* Create high-converting landing pages
* Set up automated email sequences (welcome series, reminders, upsells)
* Build sales pipelines and funnels
* Track detailed analytics on enrollments, completion, and revenue
**Example pipeline**: Free webinar → Sales page → Automatic enrollment → Onboarding emails.


### **Step 8: Test, Launch, and Optimize**

* Enroll yourself as a test student
* Check every link, video, and payment process
* Run a small beta group and collect feedback
* Launch with a promotion or early-bird offer
* Monitor performance and make improvements
**Common mistakes to avoid**:

* Putting too much content in one lesson
* Ignoring mobile optimization
* Skipping automation setup

### **Advanced Tips for Long-Term Success**

* Use Kajabi’s AI tools to generate outlines, email copy, or content ideas
* Add a community feature for ongoing engagement
* Create course bundles or membership programs for recurring revenue
* Set up an affiliate program to expand your reach

### **Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)**

**1. How much does Kajabi cost in 2026?**Kajabi offers monthly and annual plans. Annual billing gives the best rates:

* Starter: $71–$89/month
* Basic: $143/month
* Growth: $199/month
* Pro: $399/month
Discounts are often available for new users.

**2. Does Kajabi offer a free trial?**Yes. Kajabi provides a **14-day free trial** (sometimes 30 days). No credit card is required to start.

**3. Can I remove Kajabi branding?**Yes. You can remove Kajabi branding starting from the **Growth** plan and higher.

**4. How long does it take to set up a course on Kajabi?**A simple course can be set up in **3–7 days**. A fully polished course with marketing funnels usually takes 1–3 weeks.

**5. Does Kajabi have a branded mobile app?**Yes. Students can access your course through the Kajabi app on iOS and Android. Branded app experience is available on higher plans.

**6. Is Kajabi beginner-friendly?**Yes. The platform is very easy to use with drag-and-drop features, templates, and no coding required.

**7. Can I sell more than just online courses on Kajabi?**Absolutely. You can sell memberships, coaching programs, bundles, digital downloads, and more.

**8. What is the biggest limitation of the Basic plan?**Limited to 5 products and 2,500 contacts. Most growing creators upgrade to Growth.

**9. Does Kajabi integrate with other tools?**Yes. It has strong built-in tools and integrates with Zapier, ManyChat, Google Analytics, and more.

**10. Is student data and content secure on Kajabi?**Yes. Kajabi uses secure servers with login protection, drip content, and access controls.
