## Practical Deployment: Setting Up Translation Overrides in Kajabi

Configuring custom checkout labels in the modernized Kajabi admin area involves a simple four-step process:

### Step 1: Open Site Settings
Navigate to **Settings > Site Details** and scroll to the **Languages & Translations** section.

### Step 2: Select Your Target Language Profile
Choose the language you wish to customize (e.g., Spanish, German, French, or English).

### Step 3: Locate the Checkout Translations Dictionary
Expand the **Checkout & Offers** translation dictionary. Kajabi displays a two-column editor with default platform text on the left and your custom override field on the right.

### Step 4: Input Custom Phrases and Save
Replace default terms with your brand-aligned regional copy. Click **Save Changes**. Preview your checkout page in an incognito window with matching browser locale settings to verify formatting and alignment.

## Best Practices for Global Checkout Optimization

To maximize global course sales without introducing legal or operational friction:

* **Involve Native Speakers in Translation Audits:** Avoid relying solely on automated translation tools for high-stakes checkout copy. Have a native speaker review button text and guarantees.
* **Ensure Clarity on Tax and VAT Charges:** International buyers want to know upfront whether listed prices include VAT or sales tax. State tax terms clearly near the price summary.
* **Test Across Different Mobile Locales:** Emulate foreign browser headers during testing to verify that longer translated phrases do not wrap awkwardly or break button formatting on mobile screens.
