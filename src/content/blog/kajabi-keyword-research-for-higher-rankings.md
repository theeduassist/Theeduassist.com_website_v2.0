# Kajabi Keyword Research for Higher Rankings

Whether you have a Kajabi website, course, coaching program, membership, or digital product, you can attract people who are already looking for what you have to offer by appearing on Google. However, simply producing content is not enough to ensure it will be discovered.

This is where keyword research comes into play.

By using Kajabi keyword research, you can gain insights into the keywords your audience is searching for, the topics they are interested in, and the keywords that might help your content rank in search results. By combining keyword research with valuable content, effective on-page optimization, and a well-defined content strategy, you can build a more solid base for organic traffic.

In this guide, we will explore how to conduct keyword research for Kajabi, selecting keywords for courses and blogs, creating a keyword strategy for Kajabi, and leveraging keywords for improved rankings.

# What Is Kajabi Keyword Research?

Kajabi keyword research is the process of finding and analyzing the search terms people use when looking for information, products, courses, services, or solutions related to your business.

For instance, if you're an online fitness coach, you could begin with a broad keyword like “online fitness course.” By conducting keyword research, they can find more specific keywords like “online fitness course for beginners” or “online fitness program for women.”

It's not just about grabbing as many keywords as you can. The aim is to identify keywords that are relevant to your audience, align with their search intent, and are suitable for the content or offer you are developing.

A good keyword can help you determine what to write about, which page to focus on a specific search, and what information your audience is looking for.

When it comes to building a website, keyword research is essential. In the world of website building, keyword research is a crucial component.

There are numerous types of pages that can be included in a Kajabi website, such as a homepage, course pages, landing pages, sales pages, blog posts, resource pages, and more.

Every page can have a different function in your SEO strategy.

By conducting keyword research, you can discover:

• The topics that your audience is looking for.  
• The questions that potential customers are asking.  
• Long-tail keywords with more specific intent  
• Keywords related to your courses and services  
• Ideas for your Kajabi blog posts

The following are examples of opportunities to create supporting content:

• Keywords that are relevant to your niche.  
• Relevant keywords for your niche.

Rather than developing content around what you believe people are looking for, keyword research provides you with a more accurate understanding of what people are searching for.

It can also help you create a content strategy for topics that are related to each other rather than publishing unrelated blog posts.

# How to Do Keyword Research for Kajabi Step by Step

## 1. Identify Your Audience and Topic

Before searching for keywords, understand who you are creating content for.

Your audience could include:

• Online course creators  
• Students  
• Coaches  
• Consultants  
• Fitness professionals  
• Business owners  
• Educators  
• Personal development experts  
• Marketing professionals

After you know your audience, you can determine the problems, questions, goals, and topics that they might be looking for.

If you're selling a business coaching course, for instance, you might have people looking for information about starting a business, enhancing business skills, developing a business plan, or expanding an online business.

This will provide you with a place to begin your keyword research.

## 2. Start With Seed Keywords

Seed keywords are general words that relate to your topic.

For instance, a photography course developer could start with:

• photography course  
• photography lessons  
• learn photography  
• photography training

A business coach could begin with:

• business coaching  
• business course  
• online business training  
• entrepreneurship course

These general keywords can then be broken down into more specific keyword ideas.

At this point, you don't have to select your final keywords. Seed keywords are used to provide a starting point for your research.

After you have your seed keywords, search for related keywords.

You can use tools like Semrush, Ahrefs, Google Keyword Planner, and Google search suggestions to find keyword ideas.

When you're doing your Kajabi SEO keywords research, consider the following:

• Search volume  
• Keyword difficulty  
• Search intent  
• Related keywords  
• Long-tail variations  
• Questions people ask  
• Competitor keywords

Google's auto-completion feature and related searches can also help you find phrases that people are searching for.

What matters is to find the right keywords that are relevant to your audience and offer.

## 4. Analyze Keyword Difficulty and Search Volume

Search volume will tell you how often a keyword is searched, and keyword difficulty will provide you with an idea of how competitive the search term may be.

But it's not a good idea to pick a keyword just because it has a lot of search volume.

A competitive keyword can be challenging to rank for if you are a newer or smaller website.

For instance, the term “fitness” is very general. A more specific term like “online fitness course for beginners” might have fewer people, but it could be more relevant to someone seeking a specific solution.

When assessing a keyword, think about:

• Does it apply to my business?  
• Is it relevant to my audience?  
• Does the competition have a realistic nature?  
• Is there sufficient search volume for the keyword?  
• Is there a business objective that can be supported by ranking for this keyword?

The most promising keywords are those that strike a balance between relevance, search volume, competition, and business value.

## 5. Analyze Search Intent

Search intent refers to the understanding of what a user is looking to achieve when searching for a specific keyword.

There are a number of common search intents.

Informational intent is when the person is seeking information.

Examples include:

• what is Kajabi  
• How to do keyword research?  
• How to enhance website SEO?  
• What are the ways to boost website SEO?

Commercial intent is when the person is researching a product, service or solution.

Examples include:

• best online course platform  
• Kajabi SEO services  
• The best platform for online courses is Edmodo.  
• Edmodo is the best platform for online courses.

Transactional intent indicates that the individual is more likely to take an action like buying or signing up.

Examples include:

• Online marketing course is a great way to learn how to make money online.  
• enroll in business coaching program  
• online fitness course

Navigational intent is when someone is searching for a particular website, brand or page.

Knowing what people are looking for will help you produce the appropriate kind of content for each keyword.

## 6. Find Kajabi Long-Tail Keywords

Kajabi long tail keywords are longer and more specific search phrases.

Instead of targeting a broad term such as “marketing course,” you could target a more specific phrase such as “online marketing course for small business owners.”

Long-tail keywords can help you reach a more targeted audience and develop content that directly addresses a specific search.

They can also offer valuable suggestions for blog posts, FAQs, course pages, and supplementary materials.

If your primary keyword is email marketing, some of the long-tail keywords might be:

• Email marketing course for novices.  
• How to learn email marketing?  
• Email marketing training for small businesses  
• The best email marketing course for beginners is the one that suits you best.

The more specific the search, the easier it is to understand what the searcher is looking for.

## 7. Analyze Your Competitors

Competitor research can show you how other websites are targeting keywords within your topic.

Check pages that are already ranking on Google for the keywords you are targeting.

Pay attention to:

• Page titles  
• H1, H2, and H3 headings  
• Topics covered  
• Questions answered  
• Content depth  
• Internal links  
• Examples used  
• Calls to action

You can also use different [competitor analysis tools](https://www.theeduassist.com/blog/10-strong-competitor-analysis-tools-to-benchmark-your-elearning/) to understand how competing content approaches keywords, topics, and search visibility.

It's not about copying your competitors.

Rather, search for openings.

Maybe there are competing pages that don't address an important question, don't give enough examples, don't explain the topic well, or don't organize their information well.

Those gaps can provide you with opportunities to produce more valuable content.

# The Best Way to Select the Right Kajabi Keywords

Once you have a list of keywords, filter it down to a few.

## Relevance

The keyword should be directly related to your website, course, product, service, or audience.

## Search Intent

Make sure the content you plan to create matches what people expect when they search for the keyword.

## Competition

Think about if your website has a realistic chance to rank for the keyword.

## Search Demand

Check if people are searching for the topic.

## Business Value

Think about if the key word can attract visitors that are relevant to your business.

Even if a keyword has a lower search volume, it can still be valuable if it brings in the right kind of traffic.

For example:

• Course landing page → specific course topic.  
• Blog post → informational question or supporting topic  
• Service page → commercial keyword  
• FAQ page: specific questions

Assigning keywords to various pages can help avoid having multiple pages bidding for the same keyword.

It also helps your website to be more user-friendly and search engine friendly.

# How to Create a Kajabi Keyword Strategy

A good Kajabi keyword strategy can be structured with a simple framework:

The process of keyword to search intent to page type to content to internal link to measurement.

The first step is to find the keyword.

Then find out what the searcher wants.

Next, choose the appropriate page type.

Develop valuable content on the subject.

Link the page with related content using internal links.

Lastly, test the page's performance.

You can also create topic clusters of keywords.

For instance, a pillar page might be a main topic, and blog posts could address specific questions and long-tail keywords.

This will help to build a coherent content structure rather than a list of articles.

# The Most Frequent Kajabi Keyword Research Errors

If keyword research is done without a strategy, it can be less effective.

Some common mistakes include:

## Only High-Volume Keywords Are Targeted

Just because a keyword has a high search volume doesn't mean it's the best one to use.

## Ignoring Search Intent

A keyword might seem like it's relevant, but it could be bringing in visitors who are searching for something else that you don't have on your page.

## Using the Same Keyword Everywhere

Each important page should have a clear topic and purpose.

## Keyword Stuffing

Overusing keywords can make content hard to read and doesn't improve the user experience.

## Ignoring Long-Tail Keywords

Specific searches can help you reach audiences with more specific needs.

## Publishing Random Blog Posts

Your blog should be part of a larger content and keyword strategy, not a collection of random topics.

## Forgetting Internal Links

Where appropriate, related pages should be linked together so that readers can find more useful information.

# How to Track Your Kajabi SEO Keyword Performance

• Impressions  
• Clicks  
• Click-through rate  
• Average position

You can also track organic traffic and, if applicable, conversions from organic visitors.

For instance, a page can get a lot of impressions and few clicks. This may mean that the title or description of the search result should be improved.

A page can be ranked well but not have much business value if it is attracting the wrong audience.

That's why it's important to consider both SEO metrics and business goals when assessing keyword performance.

SEO is an ongoing process. Keep monitoring your rankings, traffic, content performance and user behavior, and adjust your strategy as needed.
