Imagine welcoming the people who’re interested in what you have to offer guiding the students through your courses and following up with the potential customers. You can do it without sending every email yourself. That’s what Kajabi email automation is about.

Kajabi email automation helps you send emails, reminders and follow-ups automatically based on what learners do.This makes the learning experience smoother, keeps learners engaged and reduces the work you have to do.

If you’re launching your online course or growing a training business, learning Kajabi email automation can help you build better relationships with your audience.It also saves you a lot of time.


## **What is meant by Kajabi Email Automation?**

Kajabi email automation helps you send emails automatically. This happens when users do things. For example they join your email list.. They signed up for a webinar.. They enroll in a course.

You can set up email sequences. These sequences send emails on their own. They work in the background. They help guide learners through their journey.

Here are some examples:

* A new subscriber gets an email right away.
* When someone signs up for a webinar they get reminder emails before the webinar starts.
* A student who is not really doing much with a course will get an email that says come and do some more work on the course.
The main idea of Kajabi email automation is to send the message at the right time to the people who are using Kajabi. It helps you connect with your users.


## **How does Kajabi email automation really work?**

Well it’s actually pretty simple.

Kajabi email automation uses triggers.These triggers then lead to actions.Those actions are part of email sequences.

So when you set up Kajabi email automation you choose what triggers the automation.A trigger starts the automation.

Examples consist of:

* Enrolment in courses
* Form submission
* Webinar registration
* Product purchase
Once a trigger happens Kajabi does an action that is set ahead of time.

Example given below:

The Trigger: A student joins a course

Action:

* Send welcome email
* Send onboarding guide
* Send reminder after 3 days
* Send progress check-in after 7 days
It helps learners stay updated. They do not need to ask or check manually.


## **Why is email automation important for people who create courses?**

Email automation is a help to course creators because it lets them stay in touch with the people who are learning from them.

Many people start a course and they are quite excited about it. However,  after a while they may lose interest.

If the course creators do not send them emails the learners might forget what they were supposed to do they might skip some parts of the course. They might just stop doing the course altogether.

Automated communication helps prevent this problem.

Benefits include:

Better Learner Engagement

When learners get updates they stay interested in what you are teaching and they keep coming back for more.

Improved Student Retention

Sending reminder emails and checking in on progress helps students stay focused and finish lessons.

Consistent Learner Experience

Every single learner gets this kind of support no matter when they start the course.

More Time for Content Creation

Because emails are sent automatically the people making the courses can spend their time making the courses better and helping the learners do well.

A lot of schools and organizations that teach people things want to make sure their students have a path to follow. Companies, like TheEduAssist that provide training often use straightforward ways of communicating to keep their students engaged and moving forward with their courses.

## **How Can Beginners Set Up Kajabi Email Automation?**

Setting up Kajabi email automation is simpler than most novices realise. The idea is to start with a simple process before going to more complicated automations.


### **Understand your goal**

First you need to think about what you want the automation to do for you.

Common goals include:

* Welcoming new subscribers
* Onboarding new students
* Promoting webinars
* Nurturing leads
* Recovering abandoned checkouts
A defined goal makes it easier to design effective email sequences.


### **Create an email sequence**

Now you need to go to Kajabis Marketing area.Start an email sequence here.

The sequence needs a name that makes sense so give it one, like this:

* New Student Onboarding
* Webinar Follow-Up
* Lead Nurture Campaign
* Course Completion Sequence
Clear naming makes future management much easier.


### **Send helpful emails**

Focus on assisting learners rather than promoting them.

Every email should answer one question:

What information does the learner need right now?

Useful email content may include:

* Welcome instructions
* Login guidance
* Learning tips
* Resource recommendations
* Progress reminders

### **Set Automation Triggers**

Triggers tell Kajabi when to start a sequence.

Examples involve:

* Email formatting

A simple test can prevent a poor learner experience.

A strong welcome email should include:

* A friendly introduction
* Next steps
* Course access instructions
* Support information
First impressions matter. A good welcome sequence helps build trust immediately.

* Navigation guidance

Re-engagement emails can:

## **What Are the Mistakes to Avoid with Kajabi Email Automation?**

Even good automation can fail if not properly handled.


### **Sending too much emails**

Sending many emails can be really annoying for people who subscribe to them.When you send many emails it can feel like spam.

Concentrate on making quality emails rather than sending a lot of emails.


### **Avoiding audience segmentation**

Sending identical emails to every learner often reduces engagement.

Different learners require different communication.


### **Using Generic Calls to Action**

Each email should contain one clear next step.

Example:

* Watch Lesson 2
* Download the worksheet
* Register for the webinar
Clear instructions improve results.


### **Ignoring Mobile Optimisation**

A lot of students use mobile devices to read emails.

You should always test emails on cellphones before being published. .


### **Failing to Review Analytics**

Data helps improve future campaigns.It is really hard to figure out what works and what does not work without keeping an eye on how things are going.
