## **Step-by-Step Kajabi Keyword Research Process**

### **Step 1: Define Your Audience**

First, think about who you want to reach. Specifically, ask, “What does my ideal student search for before buying?” For example, a Kajabi yoga teacher might target people who search for “gentle yoga for beginners at home.”

### **Step 2: Find Seed Keywords**

Next, brainstorm 8–10 broad terms about your course topic. Then, use Semrush or Google Autocomplete to turn those into hundreds of specific phrases. As a result, you get a full list to work from.

### **Step 3: Match Search Intent**

After that, check the intent behind each keyword. In other words, why is someone searching that phrase? Consequently, you must match your content type to that intent.

### **Step 4: Check Keyword Difficulty**

Now, filter your list by difficulty. Specifically, look for a Keyword Difficulty (KD) score under 40. Also, aim for monthly searches between 100 and 2,000. That way, you pick battles you can actually win.

### **Step 5: Pick Your Keywords**

Finally, give each page one primary keyword. Then, add two to four related secondary keywords. For instance, use “Kajabi keyword research” as primary and “Kajabi SEO tips” as secondary.

## **Best Tools for Kajabi Keyword Research**

So, which tools should you use? Here are the top four:

- **Semrush**: Best for search volume, difficulty scores, and gap analysis.
- **Google Search Console**: Shows you which queries already send traffic.
- **Google Autocomplete**: Free, fast, and based on real searches.
- **People Also Ask**: Perfect for FAQ content and featured snippets.

## **Common Mistakes to Avoid**

- **Chasing broad keywords**: Instead, go narrow first, then expand later.
- **Ignoring intent**: Otherwise, your content will not rank no matter how good it is.
- **Keyword stuffing**: On the contrary, natural use always wins over forced repetition.
- **Not tracking results**: Without data, you simply cannot improve.
