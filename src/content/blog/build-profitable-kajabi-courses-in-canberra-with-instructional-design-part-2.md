## **Designing a High‑Converting Kajabi Course Sales Funnel**

A **Kajabi course sales funnel** typically follows five stages:

* Awareness
* Lead capture
* Nurture
* Decision
* Enrollment
TheEduAssist builds this funnel directly inside Kajabi so landing pages, email sequences, and checkout flows all live in one place.


### **Typical Kajabi Course Funnel Flow**

* **Lead‑magnet landing page**: A free mini‑course, ebook, or tool is offered through a Kajabi landing page that collects emails via a Kajabi form.
* **Email automation**: A Kajabi automation sequence sends value‑packed emails that introduce the paid **Kajabi Course** and highlight student wins.
* **Kajabi sales page**: A fully branded sales page presents the course outline, pricing, bonuses, and testimonials, then guides the prospect to the checkout.
This structure keeps the journey smooth and repeatable for every new **Kajabi course** launch.


## **Community Building as a Conversion Engine:**

Kajabi lets you add **community spaces** behind your **Kajabi courses**, which TheEduAssist uses to increase engagement and retention. Community becomes both a learning hub and a subtle “sales” touchpoint, where learners see real‑time interaction and social proof.


### **How TheEduAssist Uses Community Around Kajabi Courses?**

* **Locked communities**: The community is available only to enrolled students or membership holders, increasing perceived value of the **Kajabi course**.
* **Inside‑platform discussions**: Live‑session replays, Q&A threads, and challenge updates are hosted directly in Kajabi so learners stay active.
* **Insights for future  kajabi Courses**: Community activity helps TheEduAssist refine future **Kajabi Courses**, create bundles, and design better offers.
For education brands, community is not an “add‑on”it’s a core part of the **Kajabi course** experience.


## **Email vs Social Media for Kajabi Course: Where Each Channel Fits**

For **Kajabi courses**, email and social media are complementary channels. TheEduAssist uses social media to drive traffic into the funnel and Kajabi’s email tools to convert and retain those visitors.


### **Email Marketing (Inside Kajabi):**

* Automates follow‑up sequences that send leads lesson previews, case studies, and limited‑time offers for **Kajabi courses**.
* Delivers high ROI because you own the list and can segment and personalize based on behavior.

### **Social Media Channel Plays:**

* Generates discovery content (short tips, reels, carousels, live Q&As) linked back to the **Kajabi course** landing page.
* Relies on strong organic growth or paid ads to scale, but is excellent for top‑of‑funnel visibility.
At TheEduAssist, the workflow looks like this:**Social media → Kajabi landing page → Kajabi email automation → Kajabi course checkout**.
