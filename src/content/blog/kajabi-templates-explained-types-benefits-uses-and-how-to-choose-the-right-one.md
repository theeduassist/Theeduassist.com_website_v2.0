Building a Kajabi site from scratch takes time. First, you need to pick layouts, set up pages, and test everything on mobile. So, that’s where Kajabi templates come in.

Basically, a template gives you a ready-made starting point. Then, you add your content and branding, and you’re done. In this guide, we’ll cover what Kajabi templates are, the types you can choose from, their benefits, and how to pick the best one for your business.


## **What Are Kajabi Templates?**

A Kajabi template is a pre-built website design made for the Kajabi platform. Already, it has the layout and structure in place. So, you just add your own text, images, and colors.

Overall, templates give you a strong starting point. As a result, you spend less time on design and more time on your content.


## **Why Kajabi Templates Matter for Online Businesses**

Kajabi templates help you build websites, landing pages, and course portals fast. Also, they save time, keep your design consistent, and make launching easier.


### **The Challenge of Building From Scratch**

To begin with, building a site from zero takes more than creativity. For instance, you need to know layout, color, mobile design, and what makes people click “buy.” Because of this, many creators spend weeks getting it right.

Meanwhile, every hour spent on design is an hour you don’t spend on your course or your clients. For most creators, therefore, that trade isn’t worth it.


### **How Templates Speed Up Website Creation**

Instead, a template does the hard work for you. Already, the layout is set, and the spacing is set too. So, you don’t start with a blank page.

In fact, you can go from a blank account to a live page in one afternoon. Compared to building it all by hand, that’s a big jump.


### **Why Templates Are Popular Among Creators**

Above all, templates remove the guesswork. Since the design choices are already made, you can focus on your message and your offer.

Also, templates follow proven design styles. Therefore, even creators with no design skills end up with a site that looks polished and trustworthy.

**Takeaway:** Overall, building from scratch is slow and hard. Templates, however, remove that friction so you can launch faster.


## **Types of Kajabi Templates**

Generally, Kajabi templates come in different types. Each one, however, serves a different purpose.


### **Website Templates**

Basically, these cover your whole site: homepage, about page, blog, and menu. So, use these if you want a full online presence.

If you want your template to look like a high-end digital campus, consider looking into professional [Kajabi website design and branding services](https://theeduassist.com/blog/7-powerful-kajabi-course-creation-services/)  to maximize impact.


### **Landing Page Templates**

Instead, these do one job: get sign-ups. Typically, they have a headline, a sign-up form, and little else.


### **Sales Page Templates**

On the other hand, these help turn visitors into buyers. Usually, they include benefits, reviews, pricing, and a strong call to action.


### **Course Templates**

Course templates inside the platform give you a structured shell for your lessons. ### **Membership Templates**

Similarly, these support ongoing access to content. For example, they include member dashboards and content libraries.


### **Webinar Funnel Templates**

Webinar funnel templates guide prospects through registration, thank-you, and replay pages smoothly. Since webinars convert exceptionally well, these layouts are engineered to maximize your sign-up rates.

To get the best results from these pre-built designs, map them out as part of a complete [Kajabi sales funnel setup](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://theeduassist.com/blog/kajabi-sales-funnel-setup-5-proven-ways-to-grow-enrollments/)  to consistently scale your student enrollments.

**Takeaway:** Ultimately, pick the template type based on your goal: build a brand, get leads, or sell.

### **Coaching Businesses**

Beyond speed, templates offer more. Below, here’s what else they bring.


### **Faster Launch Times**

Since most of the design is done for you, you can launch in days, not weeks.


### **Professional Design**

### **Better User Experience**

### **Lower Development Costs**

**Takeaway:** In short, templates save time, cut costs, and look professional from day one.

## **Kajabi Templates vs Building a Site From Scratch**

Below, here’s how templates compare to custom builds.

Generally, templates work well for creators who want to launch fast without high costs. On the other hand, custom builds may suit larger brands with special design needs.

**Takeaway:** For most creators, ultimately, templates offer the best mix of cost, speed, and quality.


## **How to Choose the Right Kajabi Template**

Above all, picking the right template isn’t just about looks. Instead, it’s about fit.


### **Match the Template to Your Business Model**

First, think about what you sell. Since a course creator needs different features than a coach, know your offer before you pick a layout.


### **Evaluate Customization Options**

Next, check how easy it is to change colors, fonts, and sections. Because your brand is unique, flexibility matters.


### **Check Mobile Responsiveness**

Then, test the template on a phone. Since most visitors browse on mobile, this step matters a lot.


### **Consider Future Scalability**

Also, think ahead. If you plan to add more pages or products, pick a template that can grow with you.


### **Review Support and Updates**

Finally, check if the template gets updates. So, templates that stay updated work better with new Kajabi features.

**Takeaway:** In the end, the right template fits your needs now and can grow with you later.


## **Mistakes to Avoid When Choosing a Kajabi Template**

Below, watch out for these common mistakes.


### **Choosing Based Only on Appearance**

Sometimes, a template can look great but not fit your business. Therefore, function matters as much as looks.


### **Ignoring Mobile Design**

Often, templates look great on desktop but feel cramped on phones. Since most visitors use phones, this matters.


### **Overlooking Performance**

Also, heavy templates with lots of effects can slow your page. Consequently, slow pages can hurt both visitors and rankings.


### **Buying Without Clear Goals**

Without a clear goal, it’s easy to pick the wrong template. So, know what you need the page to do before you buy.

**Takeaway:** Overall, avoid these mistakes to save time, money, and stress.

## **FAQ**


### **How do Kajabi templates work?**

Basically, you get a pre-built page or site inside your Kajabi account. Then, you replace the text, images, and colors with your own.


### **Can I customize a Kajabi template?**

Yes. In fact, you can change colors, fonts, images, and layouts to fit your brand.


### **Are premium Kajabi templates worth buying?**

For many creators, yes. Often, they have better design and more features than free templates.


### **Which Kajabi template is best for online courses?**

Generally, templates made for the course player work best. Since they include module layouts and lesson navigation, they fit courses well.


### **Do Kajabi templates affect SEO?**

Yes, in part. Specifically, fast, mobile-friendly templates tend to rank better than slow, cluttered ones.


### **Can beginners use Kajabi templates?**

Yes. Especially for beginners, templates are great, since the design work is already done.


### **What is the difference between a Kajabi template and a custom website?**

Simply, a template is ready to use and edit. A custom site, however, is built from scratch to your exact specs.


### **How much do Kajabi templates typically cost?**

Generally, prices vary. Some are free, while premium ones can cost from around $50 to a few hundred dollars.


### **Can I switch Kajabi templates later?**

Yes, in most cases. However, you may need to rebuild some pages if the new template is very different.


### **What should I look for before purchasing a Kajabi template?**

Overall, check mobile design, customization, page speed, and whether it fits your business model.
