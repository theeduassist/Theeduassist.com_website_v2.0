## Layout Psychology: Matching Interface to Intention

A major shortcoming of legacy forum software was treating every conversation identically. A short celebratory introduction had the exact same visual weight as a multi-step homework troubleshooting thread. 

Timberline allows creators to match layout architecture to user intent:


When a new member enters the `Introductions` room and sees a vibrant grid of smiling profile cards, it invites them to post their own. When they navigate to the `Technical Q&A` channel and see a clean, scannable list of answered topics, they can find solutions quickly without endless scrolling.

## Scalable Moderation: Protecting Culture as Numbers Grow

As communities expand from dozens to thousands of members, maintaining a safe, welcoming culture requires robust governance tools. Timberline introduces dedicated moderation workflows:

### 1. Granular Moderator Roles
Business owners can designate trusted alumni or assistant coaches as Community Moderators without granting them access to sales data, customer credit cards, or website page builders.

### 2. Proactive Keyword Blocklists
Automated filters intercept sensitive language, prohibited external promotional links, and spam solicitations, routing flagged submissions into a private review inbox.

### 3. Centralized Flagged Content Triage
When community members report a comment for violating guidelines, moderators can review the context, issue private warnings, or remove the post with a single click.

## Practical Setup: Customizing Spaces in Kajabi Community

To configure modern layouts inside your Kajabi Community portal:

### Step 1: Open Your Community Settings
Navigate to **Products > Community** and click the gear icon to open administrative controls.

### Step 2: Configure Space-Specific Layouts
Select an individual Space (e.g., `Wins & Celebrations`). Under the **Layout & Display** dropdown, choose your preferred format:
* Select **Card Grid** for spaces centered on visual media and personal introductions.
* Select **Feed View** for time-sensitive conversational spaces.
* Select **List View** for categorized question-and-answer databases.

### Step 3: Set Post Permissions and Pinned Guidelines
Define who can initiate topics within the room. For announcement channels, restrict post creation to admins and moderators, leaving comments open to students. Pin a friendly Community Guidelines card to the top of the feed to establish expectations.

### Step 4: Assign Moderator Badges
Under **Members**, locate your community manager or coaching assistants and assign them the **Moderator Role**. Their profiles will automatically display an official moderator flair to build trust among students.

## Best Practices for Community Leadership

To ensure your community remains an active, valuable environment:

* **Acknowledge New Members Promptly:** Ensure every introduction post receives a warm response from a moderator or instructor within 24 hours.
* **Keep Spaces Focused:** It is better to have three lively rooms than twenty silent ones. Start with fewer spaces and create new ones only when user demand requires it.
* **Seed Engaging Weekly Prompts:** Schedule recurring weekly prompts (such as `Feedback Friday` or `Wins Wednesday`) to build predictable engagement rhythms.
