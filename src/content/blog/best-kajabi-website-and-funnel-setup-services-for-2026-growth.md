## **Kajabi Website vs Other Platforms**

People often ask about the **difference between ClickFunnels and Kajabi**. ClickFunnels is excellent for standalone sales funnels and testing, but it lacks strong course hosting and membership features. Kajabi gives you the complete ecosystem website, courses, and funnels together.

You can combine both platforms when needed. To **add a Kajabi product to ClickFunnels**, use Zapier or native connections for order fulfillment and access granting. This hybrid setup works well for advanced sales while keeping content delivery inside Kajabi.

**Kajabi** is usually the better choice for education-focused businesses because it reduces complexity and improves the learner experience.
