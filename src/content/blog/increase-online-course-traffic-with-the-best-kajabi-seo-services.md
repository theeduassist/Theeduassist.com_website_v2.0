## **1. Keyword Research, Find What Your Students Are Searching**

Keyword research is the foundation of everything. Without it, you are guessing. With it, you are writing content that your ideal students are already looking for, and that Google is happy to serve them.

### **What good Kajabi keyword research looks like:**

- Use long-tail keywords like “best online nutrition course for beginners” not just “nutrition course”
- Target keywords with commercial or transactional intent, people who are ready to sign up, not just browse
- Discover your competitors on Kajabi and check out what keywords bring their traffic
- Map Keywords to Specific Pages Course Landing Page Blog Posts & About Page

The best tools for this are [Google Keyword Planner](https://ads.google.com/home/tools/keyword-planner/), [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/seo/keyword-research) and [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/features/keyword-research/). These tools can help identify keyword opportunities, search demand, competition, and related search terms.

A professional Kajabi SEO service handles this entire research process for you and builds a complete keyword map across your whole site.

## **2. On-Page SEO, Optimize Every Kajabi Page**

Kajabi lets you edit the meta title, meta description, URL slug, and image alt text on every single page. Most course creators never touch these. That is a missed ranking opportunity every single day.

### **Kajabi On-Page SEO Checklist:**

#### **Meta Title**

- Best Practice: Include primary keyword, keep under 60 chars
- Common Error: Left as Kajabi default

#### **Meta Description**

- Best Practice: Write a compelling 155-char summary with CTA
- Common Error: Left empty or auto-generated

#### **URL Slug**

- Best Practice: /online-photography-course
- Common Error: /product-abc123xyz

#### **Headings (H1–H3)**

- Best Practice: Used naturally on landing pages and blog pages
- Common Error: Multiple H1s or no headings at all

#### **Image Alt Text**

- Best Practice: Description of image + include keyword
- Common Error: Missing alt text or keyword-stuffed alt text

On-page signals are still among the most controllable ranking factors. Getting these right on your Kajabi site is low-hanging fruit with high impact.

## **3. Content Marketing and Blogging, Your Long-Term Traffic Engine**

Kajabi has a built-in blog that most creators completely ignore. A strategic blog is how you build **topical authority**, the signal that tells Google you are a credible expert in your niche, which directly leads to higher rankings.

### **How to use Kajabi’s blog for SEO:**

- Post 2-4 keyword-focused articles per month; consistency over volume.
- Each post should address a specific question your audience is actively searching for
- Link to the course landing page naturally in your blog post content
- Use your primary keyword in the H1, first 100 words, and meta description
- Internal links to other relevant posts to keep readers on your site longer

If you’re selling a personal finance course on Kajabi, topics like “How to Save $1,000 in 30 Days” or “5 Budgeting Mistakes Millennials Make” can attract people who are one click away from becoming your students.

## **4. Technical SEO, Let Google Find and Rank Your Site**

Kajabi takes care of a lot of the technical SEO for you automatically; SSL, mobile responsive, basic site structure all built in. But there are still gaps worth filling, especially when you’ve been on the platform for a while.

### **Areas of technical SEO to audit on Kajabi:**

- **Page Speed:** compress large images before uploading them. Test every important page on [Google PageSpeed Insights](https://pagespeed.web.dev/)
- **Google Search Console:** Link your Kajabi site and submit your sitemap to [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console/about) to ensure it is indexed correctly
- **Broken Links:** Use tools like [Screaming Frog SEO Spider](https://www.screamingfrog.co.uk/seo-spider/) to audit for broken links quarterly before they can impact rankings. Screaming Frog's SEO Spider can identify broken links and other technical SEO issues at scale.
- **Duplicate Content:** Use canonical tags if you have similar landing pages for the same course to prevent splitting your ranking signals
- **Mobile Experience:** Check your Kajabi theme on a mobile device; Google uses mobile-first indexing

A professional Kajabi SEO service will start with a complete technical audit and focus on fixes based on their impact on ranking.

## **5. Link Building, Build the Authority Google Respects**

Backlinks are votes of confidence from other websites. The more quality sites link to your Kajabi site, the more authority Google gives you, and the higher you will rank.

### **Practical link-building strategies for course creators:**

**Guest Posting:** Write for blogs in your niche; they link back to your Kajabi site.

**Podcast Features:** Appear as a guest on podcasts; show notes almost always include your link.

**HARO / Source Requests:** Answer journalists’ queries to earn links from major publications.

**Course Directories:** Get listed on course comparison sites and education directories.

**Creator Collaborations:** Partner with complementary course creators for mutual mentions and links.

## **6. Conversion SEO, Turn Organic Traffic Into Enrolled Students**

Getting traffic is only half the battle. The other half is making sure that traffic **converts**. Optimizing for rankings while forgetting the person actually landing on the page is one of the most common missed opportunities on Kajabi.

### **How to optimize Kajabi landing pages for conversions:**

- Write your course headline to match the search intent of your target keyword
- Include real student testimonials above the fold, social proof converts
- Add a clear, visible CTA button such as “Enroll Now” or “Start Free Trial” that does not require scrolling to find
- Use your blog posts to pre-sell the course, end every relevant post with a link to your course page
- Reduce page load time on your sales page; every second of delay costs conversions

Not all SEO agencies understand platform-specific SEO. When hiring someone to handle your Kajabi SEO, look for hands-on experience with the Kajabi platform, not just generic SEO knowledge.

### **Green flags when evaluating a Kajabi SEO service:**

- They ask about your course niche and ideal student before discussing tactics.
- They provide a content and keyword strategy, not just a list of quick fixes.
- They deliver monthly reports that show real ranking and traffic changes.
- They explain their actions in plain language, not jargon.
- They do not promise #1 rankings in 30 days; that is a red flag, not a selling point.

### **Red flags to avoid:**

- “500 backlinks for $50” offers; these rely on spammy link farms that can get your site penalized by Google
- No clear reporting or transparency about the work being done each month.
- One-size-fits-all packages with no customization for your specific niche.
- Agencies unfamiliar with Kajabi’s blogging setup or landing page structure.
