## **Introduction**

The way we train employees has really changed over the past few years. This change is largely due to microlearning.Think about how we used to train employees: they would spend hours in workshops.They would go through manuals or try to complete long boring online courses.

These methods still have their place. They are not always practical in today’s fast-paced work world.Employees are now expected to learn skills quickly. They have to balance a workload and a busy schedule.

As a result companies are looking for flexible and engaging ways to help their teams grow.Microlearning is becoming the approach for workplace training.It breaks down information into lessons that take only a few minutes to complete.

By overwhelming someone with information, microlearning lets them learn a little bit at a time.It’s no wonder so many organizations are making the switch. It’s a much better way to keep people engaged, help them actually remember what they’ve learned, and get real results.


## **The Basics of Microlearning**

Learning in parts is a great way to teach people new things. This method gives you bits of information that’re easy to understand. Each lesson is usually one thing and you can finish it in five to ten minutes.

The things you can learn from microlearning can be, in different forms, including:

* Short videos
* Interactive quizzes
* Infographics
* Podcasts
* Mobile learning modules
* Situation based exercises
* Flashcards
The goal is to make learning more accessible, convenient and engaging for employees.We want to help employees learn in a way that’s easy and fun.

Organizations can provide information in chunks.This way it fits naturally into the workday. Employees do not have to dedicate hours to training sessions.

## **Why Most Traditional Training Programs Fail**

Many organizations put a lot of money into training programs. They do not get the results they want.One of the problems is that there is just too much information.

Employees often go to training sessions and then they forget a lot of what they learned soon after.

Research has shown time and time again that people remember things better when they learn a bit at a time over a period of time rather than trying to learn everything all at once.

Traditional training can also cause problems such, as:

* Low engagement levels
* Limited knowledge retention
* Scheduling difficulties
* Makes it harder to stay productive
* Leaves learners feeling drained and tuned out
When employees feel really overwhelmed they do not want to do their job. They are more likely to forget the things they have learned about their work.

Microlearning helps with these problems. It makes training easier to handle for the employees and more fun for the employees to learn. Microlearning is a way to make sure employees remember the things they have learned about their work.


## **How Microlearning Is Making Training Better for Everyone**

The workplace today needs people to keep learning all the time.

People at work have to learn things often so they can keep up with new technologies and what is happening in their industry and what their organization needs.

It is a way that helps people learn things by giving them the information they need when they need it.

Microlearning is good because people do not have to stop what they are doing to learn something, about microlearning they can just keep going and learn about microlearning at the time.

Instead, people can learn things as part of their daily [workplace journey.](https://theeduassist.com/blog/build-ultimate-thrilling-corporate-training-that-actually-works-with-theeduassist/)

This is why Microlearning is so important for organizations that want to keep getting better.


## **1. Improves Knowledge Retention**

One big advantage of microlearning is that people remember information better.

When learners get information in chunks they can understand and recall it more easily.

Short lessons help reduce confusion and let employees focus on one idea at a time. This helps learners to remember [information](https://www.educause.edu)  and use it well at work.

Microlearning often gives results in the long run compared to traditional training methods, for organizations that use it.


## **2. Perfect for Busy Workdays**

Finding time for training is tough, for employees.They have a lot of work to do and not a lot of time to spare.

Long training courses are a problem because employees have to stop working for a time and that hurts productivity.

Microlearning is a solution because it gives employees training that they can do in just a few minutes.

Employees can do this training during their breaks. While they are waiting for a meeting to start. That makes it easy for employees to learn without messing up their daily work.


## **3. Boost Employee Engagement**

Employee engagement is a problem in workplace learning.

Long presentations and courses with much text usually don’t hold learners attention.

Microlearning uses fun and interactive content that makes learners participate.

This includes videos, quizzes, simulations and learning experiences based on real-life scenarios.These formats help keep learners motivated to keep learning.

When employees enjoy learning they are more likely to finish courses.


## **4. Supports Mobile Learning**

Today people who work are often on the move.Employees usually work because they travel a lot or they use their smartphones and tablets all day.

Mobile learning is great because people can learn things when they want to. They can take lessons from almost any place they are.

Mobile learning is really good for the people who are doing the learning. They can look at the information whenever they need to.

This means they do not have to be at their desk or in a classroom to learn something about mobile learning.Companies can also make sure everyone on the team gets the training they need with learning.


## **5. Building a Culture of Constant Learning**

Learning is something that should keep going even after you have finished the training.

Companies that do well know that helping their employees get better at their jobs is something that never really ends.

The idea of microlearning is a way to keep learning going because it gives employees a chance to learn new things and practice what they already know on a regular basis.

By just having training every now and then companies can make a plan that helps employees keep learning all year round.

This way companies can build a culture of learning and help their employees grow in their careers.


## **6. Quick  Results**

Traditional training programs take a time to get ready and to put into action. This can take weeks or even months.

Microlearning modules are different. They are faster to make and to share with people.

When new rules come out or products change companies can make learning material to help employees do their jobs better.

This way companies can stay ahead. Microlearning helps companies do this quickly.


## **7. Improves Training ROI**

Every organization wants to get the most out of the money they spend on training.

Microlearning is a way to do this because it makes training more interesting which means people are more likely to pay attention.This also means they spend time in training and more time doing their actual work.

Employees learn what they need to know in an amount of time.Microlearning helps organizations save money on developing training programs. It also works better.

So microlearning is often a better choice, than the old way of doing training because it costs less.
