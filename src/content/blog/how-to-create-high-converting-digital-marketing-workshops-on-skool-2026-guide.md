## **How Workshops Turn Into Sales (Mini Case Study)**

Many creators use workshops to sell their courses.  
They first host a free or low-cost session where they teach a useful skill. During the session, they focus on solving one clear problem and building trust.  
At the end, they offer a full course as the next step. Since the audience already got value, they are more likely to buy.  
This strategy is widely used in **online course workshops** and works better than direct selling.
