## The Global Readiness Checklist

Use this checklist to assess your digital training program before deploying it to an international audience.

### 1. Market and Audience Strategy
*   [ ] **Target Market Identified:** Have we clearly defined the specific countries and regions for the rollout?
*   [ ] **Audience Defined:** Are we training internal employees, external partners, or retail consumers? (This impacts data privacy rules).
*   [ ] **Language Selected:** Have we identified the specific dialects required? (e.g., Brazilian Spanish vs. European Spanish; Simplified vs. Traditional Chinese).

### 2. Localization and Cultural Adaptation
*   [ ] **Terminology Glossary:** Has a glossary of product terms and company jargon been approved by a local Subject Matter Expert (SME)?
*   [ ] **Cultural Audit:** Have images, scenarios, and colors been reviewed for cultural appropriateness in the target region?
*   [ ] **Format Adaptation:** Are dates, times, currencies, and measurements formatted correctly for the local market?

### 3. Technical Implementation
*   [ ] **Text Expansion:** Have course layouts been adjusted to accommodate languages that expand (like German) or contract?
*   [ ] **RTL Support:** If translating to Arabic or Hebrew, does the authoring tool and LMS fully support Right-to-Left formatting?
*   [ ] **Font Rendering:** Have appropriate, web-safe local fonts been embedded so characters display correctly?
*   [ ] **Multimedia:** Have videos been subtitled or dubbed? Are external video hosts (like YouTube) accessible in the target country (e.g., China)?

### 4. Accessibility
*   [ ] **Standard Identified:** Have we identified the required accessibility standard for the target market? (e.g., Section 508 in the US; EAA in Europe; WCAG 2.1 AA generally).
*   [ ] **Keyboard Navigation:** Is the localized course fully navigable without a mouse?
*   [ ] **Screen Reader Support:** Does the course support assistive technologies in the target language?
*   [ ] **Contrast and Alt Text:** Are contrast ratios compliant and are localized alt-texts provided for images?

### 5. LMS and Learner Data Privacy
*   [ ] **Platform Latency:** Have we tested the LMS load times from within the target country?
*   [ ] **Data Mapping:** Do we know exactly what personal data the LMS and SCORM package are collecting?
*   [ ] **Privacy Law Assessment:** Have we reviewed our data collection against regional laws (e.g., GDPR, PIPL, PDPL, CCPA)?
*   [ ] **Data Hosting Location:** Is the LMS server located in a compliant jurisdiction? Do we have legal mechanisms in place for cross-border data transfers?
*   [ ] **Regulatory Bodies:** Does the specific market require external licensing or approval (e.g., the NeLC in Saudi Arabia)?

### 6. Quality Assurance (QA)
*   [ ] **Linguistic QA:** Has a native speaker reviewed the course in-context to verify translation accuracy?
*   [ ] **Visual QA:** Has the course been checked for broken layouts, cut-off text, or misaligned graphics?
*   [ ] **Functional QA:** Do all buttons, variables, and interactions work correctly in the localized version?
*   [ ] **LMS QA:** Has the localized SCORM package been uploaded to a staging environment and tested for accurate completion tracking and scoring?

### 7. Launch and Maintenance
*   [ ] **Pilot Phase:** Have we conducted a pilot launch with a small subset of the target audience?
*   [ ] **Feedback Loop:** Is there a mechanism for local learners to report translation errors or technical bugs?
*   [ ] **Version Control:** Do we have a system to trigger translation updates when the English master course changes?
